# The light kit: the treatments, the fences, the recipe, the plan

> **ROLE:** the `light` exploration's proposal after four rounds (the review wave, 2026-09-14 to 15),
> kept here so it outlives the track manifest. **NOT LAW** until Will rules; the board is
> `/design/c/light` while it stands, and every value below is generated by the board's own code from
> one source (`sandbox/light/kit.ts` and `candidates.ts`), never typed twice. The record of each round
> is the CHANGELOG's review-wave entry.

Three explorations set this identity, and round four makes them one kit. The **spill doctrine**
(`sandbox/glow-doctrine-variants.tsx`) named five shapes the engine can make and proved them on
production's real grounds. The **spill placements** (`glow-moments-variants.tsx`) decided where a light
is earned, fourteen placements with four shipped and four turned down with reasons, and wrote the
beam's four laws and the scarcity distance. This board asked what the whole system would be if it were
designed today.

**A shape is not a treatment.** A treatment is a shape, at a place, at a register, at a cadence, on a
named kind of section, with a mount you can paste. That is what the three become together, and it is
what a wiring round cuts from.

**The asks, one word each (the board's Rule on panel):**

- The kit, as written: land it, or name what to change.
- The infusion plan: begin at phase 1, or name the order.
- The aurora: no, the seam, both boundaries, or the room.
- Its register: accent or identity.
- Depth in dark: the family (lift and float), lift only, or neither.
- The lit face: adopt, adapt or drop.
- The cadence: 8s or 11s.
- The paper five: hand-tuned, the flat row, or the dark set.
- The publish beat: 300 as shipped, the house five, or 305.

## One doctrine, three jobs

Organised around what the light is DOING, not where it comes from. The system today is organised the
other way, and three consequences are visible in the repo: the footer's seam, the lamp Will named as
the model, is illegal under the law that opens the doctrine; dark has no shadow even where two
photographs plainly need one; and the ring lift, the fourth depth technique with 77 uses, is in no
document at all.

| Job | What it is | Treatments |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Separate** | Achromatic, static. It says one object is in front of another. This is the elevation contract, and it belongs INSIDE the light doctrine rather than beside it | the step, the ring, lift, float, the lit face |
| **Fill** | Chromatic, slow, always behind content. It gives a room a temperature | the seam, the throw, the aurora |
| **Mark** | Chromatic, bounded, and it ends when its state ends | the sweep, the bloom, the halo, the beam |

**The rule that replaces "name the lamp, and if you cannot point at the object emitting there is no
spill":** a lamp needs a **PLACE**, not an object. An edge, a boundary, a screen, a plate, a horizon.
It reaches every verdict the old law reached (a pill's rim is not a place, a nav panel is not a place,
a skeleton is an absence) and it admits the footer seam, which the old law forbade and which is the
lamp the whole system is calibrated against.

## The twelve treatments

### Separate (the depth cues)

| Treatment | What it is | Its place | How often |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The step** | A surface one token lighter than the one under it. The cheapest separation there is, and the only one that costs no paint | any surface that sits on another | always, and first: nothing else is reached for until the step is not enough |
| **The ring** | A hairline that states an edge without implying height. `ring-1 ring-foreground/5` on surfaces, `/10` on media frames. 77 uses, and until this board in no document | the edge of a surface or a media frame | always, beside the step |
| **Lift** | The smaller of the two shadows. It separates two objects of the SAME lightness that overlap | an overlap between objects that are equally light | wherever the overlap exists, in both modes |
| **Float** | The same geometry at double the offsets. It detaches a LAYER from content that keeps living behind it | a layer over content: menu, dialog, sheet, popover, toast | only on a layer. A flat surface takes neither shadow, in either mode |
| **The lit face** | An inset hairline plus a one-pixel lip. Not elevation at all: MATERIAL, a face catching light | a media frame, a screen, a plate | on the three surfaces the doctrine names, and nowhere else |

### Fill (the fields)

| Treatment | What it is | Its place | How often | Ships |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The seam** | Spill across a boundary: a band at full strength where the edge is, falling away from it | a boundary between two grounds, or an object's own bottom edge | once or twice a page, a scarcity distance apart | `footer-glow.tsx`, `film-strip-glow.tsx`, `screen-lamp.tsx` |
| **The throw** | An origin-anchored cast, thrown outward from a point on the object. It replaces a rim, because a rim has no vector | a plate, a card or a screen sitting on open dark | rare: one per page at most | not built |
| **The aurora** | The fill job at CHAPTER scale: the house light itself, at rest, in a section with no media. A register, a placement grammar and a clock, not a lamp | a chapter's own two boundaries, never its middle | one chapter per page at accent; every chapter only at identity | not built |

### Mark (the moments)

| Treatment | What it is | Its place | How often | Ships |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The sweep** | The comet plus the phase-locked edge ring: the half of the recipe we never adopted | an object arriving, at the moment it arrives | a moment, never a state | not built (the engine has it) |
| **The bloom** | A one-shot that decays to the base and never to zero, so the object stays lit after the beat | a moment that just happened, on the object it happened to | once per session at the very most | `qr-hero.tsx` (the plate's ignition, resting at 0.34) |
| **The halo** | An object lit from BEHIND: the mask clears its own centre, so colour creeps in from the rim and the face stays clean | behind an object that must stay legible while its surround lights | the standing exception: one per page, and it has to be argued | not built |
| **The beam** | An object lit BECAUSE IT IS the live subject. Not the engine: the vendored border beam at the live register of the same five hues | the object itself, while its state runs | one subject per view | `pro-card-beam.tsx` |

**Half the kit already runs in production and nobody had written down what it was called.** That is the
strongest fact the board found.

**Two corrections the board made to its own first draft.** The QR plate's light is a BLOOM, not a
throw: centred at 50 by 50, no vector at all, decaying to a resting 0.34. Under law 2 a vectorless
field is the even rim the doctrine refuses, and it is admitted because it is a MARK and not a fill (the
plate is lit because the code is live). Naming which job a light is doing is what makes both legal.
And the halo needs a dark object: our primary action is white, so the halo can light a secondary action
and cannot light the primary one, which moves its place from the call to action at the top of a page to
an action at the end of a flow.

## The fences, and the case behind each

A fence with no case behind it is an opinion. Every line is either a law the three explorations ruled,
or something a specimen on the board falsified.

- **Never the middle of a section.** The copy then sits IN the light instead of in the clean band
  between two of them. It is on the board as a specimen rather than as a warning, because this is the
  half of the grammar a wiring round is most likely to get wrong.
- **Never a fill behind everything.** globals.css says it in its own words: a seam is a band, not a
  fill, and generalising that away is what turns spill into a wash sitting on the copy.
- **Never a rim, a concentric halo, or an even ring.** Law 2: light has a vector and every instance
  declares it. The container mask is origin-anchored, so the engine has no even-rim mode to reach for.
- **Never on a nav panel, a dropdown, the storage meter near its cap, an upload error, a generic
  skeleton, a CtaBand, or anywhere in the admin portal.** The moment spill can mean warning it is a
  state colour and the system is decoration. Failure is `--destructive`, full stop.
- **Never a house token, never a state colour, never a sixth hue.** Bible 3: the lamp set is light,
  never UI. A temperature is a re-ordering and a narrowing of the same five. A moment may lean the five
  toward the nearest of them (300 becomes 305); it may not introduce one.
- **Never a lamp inside an `overflow-hidden` ancestor.** The blurred falloff clips to a hard rectangle,
  which reads as a grey box and got the album straddle reverted. Check for clipping ancestors before
  placing a lamp: this repo has several.
- **Never a light box that ends on screen.** The five ellipses still have opacity at the field's left
  and right extremes, so a box that ends mid-viewport ends the light on a straight vertical cut. Go
  full-bleed, or pool it with a radial mask on the WRAPPER.
- **Never two lamps inside one viewport of scroll.** Scarcity is a distance, not a count. It governs
  LAMPS; a section carrying a field is not a lamp for this purpose, which is why the register matters.
- **Never a band without its base.** A swept layer rests fully off-layer, so a band-only lamp is
  invisible whenever it is paused, which is its default state below the fold AND its reduced-motion
  state.
- **Never the hero.** Measured, not preferred: the reverted lamp round put a light on the hero and it
  failed three of the four things a lamp needs. A light needs a quiet ground to fall on, and the hero's
  ground is twenty four drifting photographs.

## What a wiring round lands

The bill of materials. Not one of these is a research finding: each row is a thing somebody types into
a file, and the whole of it is seven rows.

| What | Where | Is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--shadow-lift`, `--shadow-layer` | globals.css beside `--shadow-float`; theme.css maps them | One geometry, two sizes, one alpha ramp per ground. Paper keeps today's bytes exactly; dark gains the ramp it never had (0.45 / 0.55 and 0.50 / 0.62) |
| `[data-lit]`, with its paper half | globals.css; the three surfaces take the attribute | The lit face, promoted out of `glow-lab.css`, with the bottom-edge variant paper has always lacked |
| `--aurora-cadence` | globals.css, as `calc(var(--spill-cadence) * 3)` | A SIBLING of the lamp's clock. A lamp keeps the lamp's clock; a field takes a multiple of it. The ratio is the proposal, and the cadence ruling picks what it multiplies |
| `--lamp-1..5` on `.surface-paper` | globals.css, a second declaration of the same five hues | The hand-tuned paper register. 85 and 155 lifted and desaturated, 255 and 305 left to carry the chroma |
| `glw-drift-x`'s from-keyframe, outside the reduced-motion block | globals.css, the engine | One line, and a law 4 fix rather than a feature: the transform drive's rest state is currently the comet parked dead centre at full strength. Nobody has seen it because no shipped lamp uses that drive, and the aurora is the first thing that should |
| `<SectionLight>` | `src/components/marketing/system/section-light.tsx`, beside `screen-lamp.tsx` | The aurora's mount: placement, register and temperature in one place, so a chapter asks for light rather than assembling two bands and four custom properties. The board's composer exports its exact call for every configuration |
| A grain tile | `public/design/`, referenced by the aurora's own rule | A field this large at this alpha bands in 8 bits; the engine's warp displaces the colour but adds no entropy. Requested from Will; the board ships a generated stand-in meanwhile |

### The aurora's recipe

```
<SectionLight placement="both" register="accent" temperature="house">
  <NoApp />
</SectionLight>
```

Two seams at the section's own boundaries, the bottom one flipped on its own axis, each 42 percent of
the section's height, on the transform drive. The engine has no bottom-seam shape and should not grow
one: the geometry is identical and only the vector differs, and a vector is the caller's to turn.

| Register | Cinema (base / band) | Paper (base / band) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Accent** (one section a page) | 0.30 / 0.13 | 0.52 / 0.24 |
| **Identity** (every section) | 0.17 / 0.07 | 0.30 / 0.13 |

★ **Paper takes HIGHER numbers than cinema, not lower**, which is the opposite of the instinct: a tint
at l 0.88 against a near-white page has far less contrast with its ground than the same tint has
against `oklch(0.11)`. Blur is 38px at chapter scale (16px on a 210px seam is the same softness as
roughly 38px on a 560px one, and `--glw-blur` is a literal, not a ratio).

## The infusion plan

Six phases, and the order is the whole proposal. Achromatic first, because the separate job has no
colour to collide with and every other phase is judged badly until it lands. One page before every
page, because "one accent section per page" only means something where a page has a ruled chapter map,
and exactly one page does. And a phase that needs another board's ruling waits for it: two do, and
landing them early would mean landing them twice.

| # | Phase | Lands | Where | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **The floor** | The separate job, whole: the shadow family with its per-ground alphas, the ring written into the elevation contract, `[data-lit]` on the three surfaces | globals.css and the floating primitives. Every page, marketing and app | Nothing. It is already wearable on the real site from the board's Apply button |
| 2 | **The two ends of the home arc** | `<SectionLight>`, `--aurora-cadence`, and the aurora at accent on two sections: the guest ledger and the closer | `/` only. Two call sites | The grain tile (stand-in meanwhile) and the cadence ruling |
| 3 | **Paper** | `--lamp-*` on `.surface-paper`, then the aurora on the paper chapters | `/`'s paper chapter, `/help`, `/contact`, the pricing page's paper band | The palette board's paper ramp first, or the same merge: a lighter ground needs MORE alpha, so a register tuned against today's paper is wrong the moment the ramp moves |
| 4 | **The feature pages** | The seam on every feature hero with a screen, the throw on plates that are present rather than live, the lit face on the plates | `/features/*` | The media kit's feature-page art. The sampler must be chosen deliberately at every new call site: the DOM form taints on a raw presigned tile and silently returns the fallback five |
| 5 | **The moments** | The bloom on the publish beat at the leaned five; the sweep on an arrival | the reel's publish moment; the guest upload landing | The publish beat's colour ruling. A moment touches app state, so it needs wiring rather than a CSS block |
| 6 | **The app** | Phase 1 already reaches it. After that, the one bloom the app earns (the first event ever created) and nothing else | `/dashboard`, the event page | The app's own design round. Light arrives here last and thinnest, on purpose: these are the screens a host opens every day |

**Begin with the floor.** It is the only phase that needs nothing from any other board, it is already
wearable on the real site, and it is the one phase whose absence makes every other phase harder to
judge. Phase 2 is two call sites on one page after that, which is a deliberately small second step: the
closer above the footer is the hardest test the scarcity distance has anywhere on the site, and it
should be taken while the cost of being wrong is two lines.

## The doctrine's replacement text

The board's last block carries the exact markdown that replaces each block of `design-system.md`,
headed by the line that stands there today, plus the five CSS blocks for `globals.css` (the same bytes
the board's Apply buttons hand the browser). A ruling is a few words and the Orchestrator pastes rather
than rewrites.

**What goes.** "If you cannot point at the object emitting, there is no spill" (replaced by the place).
"One depth technique per mode" and "Dark: NO shadows anywhere" (replaced by one cue per relationship).
"Never a violet reel glow" as an absolute (replaced by: light never takes its colour from a meaning,
but a moment may lean the five toward the nearest hue, so 300 becomes 305). The Paper register's "a
hand-tuned paper five is still an open design task" (done).

**What stays.** The lamp set is light, never UI (bible 3), and its fence in
`globals-theme-contract.test.ts`. Direction, colour and falloff, word for word. BEAM's four laws and
the standing Get Pro exception. Scarcity as a distance. The four-question LampCard, with question one
restated as the place. Every animation inside the reduced-motion block, and every lamp's rest state
designed.

## A lab defect this round found, and it is not the light's

Round four mounted nine live production sections, which is what Will asked every board for, and that
surfaced a cascade order bug in the lab itself. The lab's own Tailwind entry
(`src/app/(dev)/design/design.css`) emits into `layer(utilities)` and loads AFTER `globals.css`, so at
equal specificity an unprefixed utility from the lab sheet outranks a responsive one from the
production sheet: `grid-cols-1` beats `lg:grid-cols-12`, `hidden` beats `sm:block`. **Every board that
mounts a production component is therefore showing parts of it in its phone layout at 1440.** Measured
on the light board: 115 elements across the nine sections change geometry when that sheet is removed,
and it reproduces on a production build, so it is not a dev artifact.

The remedy is one character, verified in the browser: emit into `layer(utilities.lab)` instead, so the
lab's utilities sit in a sub-layer of `utilities` and lose to production's rules, which are in the
layer directly. `design.css` is outside every track's lane, so this is the Orchestrator's to land.
