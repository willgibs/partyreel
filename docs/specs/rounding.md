# The rounding: six families, and the four calls a family does not settle

> **ROLE:** the `rounding` exploration's standing proposal, kept here so it outlives the track manifest.
> **NOT LAW** until Will rules; the board is `/design/lab/rounding` while it stands, and every paste it
> offers is generated by the board's own code, never typed by hand.
>
> **What is not here:** the asks, the six families' own copy and the arithmetic. They live in exactly one
> place, the board's spec (`src/app/(dev)/design/sandbox/rounding/spec.ts`), which the board, the desk's
> "Waiting on you", the review panel's message and `pnpm lab:review`'s validator all read. A second copy
> could only go stale, and a ruling recorded against a stale option is worse than no ruling. This file is
> what is RULED and what is OPEN, and nothing else: no ledger, no history (git holds that).

## The recommendation

**C, soft.** An 8px card, a 12px menu, a 4px photograph, with the button left where it ships.
The seven derived steps climb in even quarters, the top two are deleted, and the gap between
photographs follows the photograph.

At 8px a card finally has a corner you can see, and a 16px button is still twice as round as the
surface under it, so a control still reads as the pressable thing. What is broken is not the button.
If a photograph may not be cropped by a corner at all, **E, print** is C with the photograph at zero
and nothing else moved, and it closes the album's gap by deletion.

The paste is generated from whatever the dock is claiming, so it is never typed here: pick a family
and copy the block under "One real page, wearing the pick". It lands on `:root` alone
(`globals.css` says never to alias the radius tokens to `.surface-paper`), and the ladder rides along
as utility overrides because `@theme inline` bakes each step into its utility at build time.

## What is open

1. **The button rung.** Today's 0.4 of the height, a full pill, or half of today. The board says today.
2. **The seven steps.** Today's multipliers or even quarters. The board says quarters.
3. **The two dead rungs.** Three uses between them, one of which is the Badge faking a pill. The board
   says drop.
4. **The album's gap.** Pinned to the photograph's corner, or left at the literal 3px. The board says
   pin, and a yes is also a yes to scheduling the sweep.

## What a ruling costs, whichever way it goes

- **Sixty-four corners on the site are literals, not tokens.** `rounded-[2px]`, `-[3px]` and `-[4px]`
  account for 52 across 24 non-lab files, nearly all of them photographs. Under any family but A and F
  a photograph keeps today's corner while the card around it moves, so a ruling is also a sweep.
- **The guest album's gap is a literal in three files.** `guest-masonry.tsx`, `gallery-skeleton.tsx` and
  `ghost-grid.tsx` write `gap-[3px]` while their tiles ride `var(--radius-tile)`, so any corner above 3
  opens holes where four corners meet, on the one grid every guest sees.
- **The guest entry sheet wears the button token.** `entry-shell.tsx` draws the first surface any guest
  meets at 1.4x `--radius-action`, so the button rung decides the corner of a sheet. It should move to
  the floating layer's token.
- **`--radius-action-lg` has one call site, on an h-11.** Every marketing CTA is `size="lg"` forced to
  h-11 in 26 files, so the loudest action on the site sits at 0.33 of its height while `globals.css`
  documents 0.4. The proposal is a `cta` size on the Button at 1.1x `--radius-action`, and `-lg` retires.
- **The float corner is ruled on two boards.** This one sets `--radius-float`; the `floating-surfaces`
  proposal adds `--radius-float-item` and `--radius-float-lg`. They have to agree.
- **The ladder is not a token.** `--radius-xl` is empty at runtime, so the retune lands on the
  multipliers in `theme.css`, one line a step.
