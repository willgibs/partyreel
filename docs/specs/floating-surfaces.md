**The asks live in the board, not here.** They are `asks` in
`src/app/(dev)/design/sandbox/floating-surfaces/spec.ts`, which is the one list the answer block, each

## What is proposed

**One of seven finished layers, and the board's own answer is Card.** A floating surface is four
decisions: how round it is, how it arrives, what it is made of, and whether it casts. The seven are
Today (the floor), Card (a menu as a made object: a title, labelled groups, an icon rail, state on the
right, the undoable action under its own rule), Glass (one translucent pane of the room), Command (a
field instead of a list), Compact (the same menu at 26px rows with a group's name riding its rule),
Paper (no shadow at all, a real border, the squarest corner) and Lift (no edge at all, a doubled
shadow doing the whole job). Card is proposed because it answers the complaint the review named, on all
ten surfaces at once; Compact is the overrule if a two-row overflow becoming furniture is the thing
that cannot be lived with.

**A layer is two halves, and only one is a paste.** Material, radius, motion and density are CSS over
the primitives' own data-slots, so they reach the real pages today. The anatomy (a header row, an icon
rail, a footer rail, a group rule) and the model (a field, no submenu) are components: a ruling for
Card, Compact or Command is an edit to `src/components/ui/dropdown-menu.tsx` in the wiring round.

**The family is TEN surfaces, not nine.** `guest/entry-shell.tsx` renders a raw vaul drawer that never
goes through `ui/drawer.tsx`, with a literal radius, and it is the floating surface most people on this
product will ever see. Every layer reaches it through `[data-entry-drawer]`.

## What is still open

1. **The nested menu.** Delete it, choices inline, is proposed. One menu in the product opens another:
   the theme picker inside the account menu, on every host page and every guest page.
2. **The corner.** Today's panel with the rows corrected is proposed: the 8px panel stays and each row
   rises 4px, so the two share a centre (bible 9). Card already is this corner.
3. **How it appears.** Faster where you open often is proposed, which reads rule 15 as one entrance
   LANGUAGE with rule 12 setting the clock inside it. That wording change is Will's to make.
4. **The shadow in dark.** A soft shadow is proposed, and it is the light board's own float shadow to
   the byte, so the line is ruled once on that board and inherited here.

## The standing findings

- **A shipped bug, true whatever is ruled.** A nested submenu is invisible in the product:
  `dropdown-menu.tsx` renders `SubContent` with no `Portal` while `Content` carries `overflow-y-auto`,
  so the submenu is a descendant of a box that clips it. It opens at the right place, computes visible
  at opacity 1, and paints nothing. The one call site is the account menu's theme picker, which host
  and guest both carry. Deleting the branch takes the bug with it; keeping it is a ruling to add the
  missing wrapper.
- **Bible 9 is missed inside the shipped contract.** An 8px panel around 1.6px rows sitting in 4px of
  padding does not nest, by a factor of six, measured off the live DOM at six times magnification.
- **The reduced-motion gate is the standing hole.** `globals.css` has clamped every animation to
  0.01ms under the preference since 2026-06-11, but the primitives carry no block of their own, which
  is bible 14's first line. Every block this board generates is guarded; the patch for the primitives
  lands with whichever layer wins.
