# The type scale: five finished ladders, and the two calls left after one is picked

> **ROLE:** the `type-scale` exploration's standing proposal, kept here so it outlives the track
> manifest. **NOT LAW** until Will rules; the board is `/design/lab/type-scale` while it stands, and
> every paste it offers is generated by the board's own code, never typed by hand.
>
> **The asks, the cards and their numbers are NOT here.** They live in exactly one place, the board's
> own spec and its data (`src/app/(dev)/design/sandbox/type-scale/spec.ts` and `ladders.ts`), which
> the board, the desk's "Waiting on you", the review panel's message and `pnpm lab:review`'s

## The recommendation

**B, rungs, on both halves of the site, with the dead-link heading brought onto the same set.**

One rung set from 12 to 160 with the ratio widening as it climbs, so every heading has a size at both
ends of the screen and reads its line spacing and letter spacing off that size instead of choosing
them. Today's desktop sizes are an unevenly rounded draft of the same set (160 / 96 / 72 / 60 / 48 is
roughly 160 / 100 / 80 / 64 / 52), and the app gains the middle size it has never had. The law under
it is the travel: marketing moves four rungs between 375 and 1440, the app moves one, the card step
moves none, which is bible 2 stated as arithmetic rather than judgement.

**What would change it:** C, registers, if the public pages should read like a poster (200 over 120
rather than 160 over 100) and the signed-in app should go quieter than today rather than louder.

## What is settled, whichever card wins

- **One token set, two registers.** `display` through `prose` are marketing's rungs and `page`
  through `card` are the app's, named once. Two separate sets would name every role twice and then
  have to answer which set a `CardTitle` wears, since it is one component shipping on /pricing and on
  the dashboard, and they would duplicate a letter-spacing law that is a function of size, not of
  surface. A ruling that wants marketing from one card and the app from another is still one
  `@theme` block: each half stands on its own rungs.
- **No app heading goes below the body text it sits above** (14px, the size a `Card` sets on its own
  subtree). Below that line the hierarchy is carried by weight and colour alone.
- **A step is a pair, not a number.** Every card declares what it is at 375 and at 1440, and a wiring
  round bakes the pair as one `clamp()` through both points, which removes the four-breakpoint ramps.

## What is open

Two questions, both on the board with their context and their options:

1. **Letter spacing by size** (`tracking`). Four of the five cards make letter spacing and line
   spacing a function of size. It moves no size, so it can be taken whichever card wins, and it is
   the only one of today's faults that today's numbers can fix by themselves.
2. **The dead-link heading** (`not-found`). The one page title on the site set in Inter, the body
   font, at a size no card reaches. Every card's paste puts it on the set, at the app's page step
   inside the app and at the prose step on marketing.

## What a wiring round inherits

- **The sweep is four headings no hook can reach**, and all four have to move with the ruling: the
  app's section heading (an 11px uppercase label inside an `h2` on the dashboard and the event feed,
  14px in admin, no class worth aiming at); sixteen hand-rolled marketing headings at 30 / 36 that
  stop one rung short of `SectionShell`'s ramp; the guest entry title, written inline as
  `font-heading text-[28px]`; and the event name inside the production `EventCard`, a hand-rolled
  `font-heading text-xl`. None can be a HOOK, because aiming a step at one would move the real site
  under the Today card, which is the control the board rests on.
- **The bake is one `@theme` block**, and a baked step becomes one utility class, so the ramps in
  `page-hero`, `section-shell` and `page-heading` collapse to one class each. `--tracking-tight` goes
  with them.
- **Only the root 404 is outside every design island** (`app/not-found.tsx` renders outside both
  marketing and the app), so a paste reaches every other surface in a real tab.
