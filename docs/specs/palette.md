# The palette: the theming model, the ramps, the accent and the panel, as proposed

> **ROLE:** the `palette` exploration's proposal after five rounds (the review wave, 2026-09-14 to 15),
> kept here so it outlives the track manifest. **NOT LAW** until Will rules; the board is
> `/design/lab/palette` while it stands, and every paste below is generated by the board's own code, never
> typed by hand. The record of each round is the CHANGELOG's review-wave entry.
>
> **The asks are not here any more.** Since round five they live in exactly one place, the board's own spec
> (`src/app/(dev)/design/sandbox/palette/spec.ts`), which the board, the desk's "Waiting on you", the
> review panel's message and `pnpm lab:review`'s validator all read. A second copy in this doc could only
> go stale, and a ruling recorded against a stale option is worse than no ruling: read them on the board,
> or on `/design/lab`.

**Round five.** The board moved onto the lab kit's template, which changed what a reviewer meets first
rather than what the board argues: the model, the eight one-word calls and the case are now the opening
screen instead of the closing one, and the thirteen sections carry the evidence under the ask each
answers. Three pieces of it got better on the way. The live sections became live PAGES: the four
production sections this board used to portal into an iframe of its own making retired to the kit's
Frame loading the real routes, today on the left and the pair on the right, scrolled together and wearing
the exact block the Apply button hands the site, so the footer seam and the accent on the mark are judged
on the page rather than on a drawing of it. The today-beside-the-candidate frame became a wipe, one
canvas with the seam on a slider, so a 0.02 step is compared across four pixels instead of across a gap.
And the two set cards became two tables where the row IS the dock control, so comparing two darks stopped
being a press, a scroll and a memory test. No set, number or recommendation changed.

**Round four.** Round four stopped adding candidates and named the
system instead. Cinema and ink turned out to be one mode's two grounds rather than two darks: the
room a dark chapter sits in, and the slab a dark leaf makes on a light page. Light has the same pair
(the paper, and the mat, which is the /contact panel nobody had named), and the media well belongs to
neither mode because it is always dark. Four registers and a bed, where the product had five unnamed
grounds and a literal. The board's first block states it and it is the first ask. The ruling then
split in two, as Will asked: six dark sets beside five light ones, thirty pairs, one paste from
whichever pair is up, and three new darks (a warm room, a cool one, and one lifted off black that
argues dark needs a single register) beside two new papers. The split immediately produced an answer
no earlier round could have reached, because warming had only ever been askable about both modes at
once: warm the room, leave the page a true grey. Every page-wide switch moved into the dock, and the
comparison surfaces became live production sections, rendered in unscaled iframes at the canvas width
so a real breakpoint measures the canvas. Lab only, no production byte.
