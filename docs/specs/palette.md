# The palette: the ramp, the accent and the panel, as proposed

> **ROLE:** the palette exploration's proposal (the review wave, 2026-09-14; `lp/palette`,
> integrated `d5847ad`), kept here so it outlives the track manifest: three complete candidate token
> sets, each a paste for `:root`, `.dark`, the gallery canvas, `.surface-ink` and the cinema ground.
> **NOT LAW** until ruled; bible 1 inherits the ruled ramp and accent, and the Orchestrator pastes the
> ruled block into `globals.css` and `marketing.css`. The board is `/design/c/palette` while it
> stands; the blocks are generated by `tokenBlock()` in `sandbox/palette/ramps.ts`.

**The asks on the board:** the ramp (A one ladder, B one room, C film stock, or today's, in both
modes); the accent (which hue: ink today, blue 252, violet 300, flare 330; and which of its three
jobs it takes: identity, attention, the stand-in for media); the panel (one token at full strength,
retiring the six alphas it ships at, hover fills moving to `--secondary`); the dark grounds (three
steps of one ladder, A and C, or one room for cinema, the app, ink and the canvas, B); the missing
step (`--faint` enters the token set, or the 37 alpha-dimmed text sites stay as they are).

**The findings:** the light middle is empty and the 20 `text-muted-foreground/70` sites composite to
exactly the missing step; the dark panel ships LIGHTER than the card it sits in (0.245 over 0.21);
`.surface-ink` has no `--card`, so a card in the footer is near white; and `--gallery` is doing two
jobs (a lightbox wants the deepest surface, a footer leaf wants a slab that reads on paper), which
makes bible 16's four grounds five. A and C separate them (canvas 0.09, ink 0.185); B makes every
dark surface one room. Either way rule 16 is counting wrong: Will's to rule.

**The departures flagged:** C re-opens the zero-chroma decision `globals.css` records as closed
(0.003 to 0.008 warm chroma, on the board rather than in a comment); A and C make the dark card
opaque, retiring the one translucent surface (only B keeps the veil); B deletes the cinema override
in `marketing.css`, so the cinema-to-footer seam belongs entirely to light; all three complete
`.surface-ink`; each adds `--faint`, which needs `--color-faint: var(--faint);` in `theme.css`'s
`@theme inline` block before a `text-faint` utility exists.

## The three token blocks (the paste)

The ruling is a candidate letter; these are what lands. Each block is generated by the board itself
(`tokenBlock()` in `ramps.ts`), so what is written here is what row 10 renders. `--faint` is new in
all three and needs one line in `theme.css`'s `@theme inline` block (`--color-faint: var(--faint);`)
before a `text-faint` utility exists.
