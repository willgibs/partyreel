# lp/publish-bloom

**Goal.** A wiring round, not an exploration: the moment a host shares a reel joins the Aurora. Today
it is two flat violet box-shadow animations (`rxp-pubglow` breathing INSIDE the studio's reel frame,
`rxp-bloom` riding out of the share card on the host's event page), both decaying to nothing. Will
ruled both halves (`docs/reviews/light.json` round 7; `docs/design/rulings.md`, the 2026-09-17
sections):

- the colour, `publish=house-five`: "Let's keep it consistent with the rest of our glows. Don't need a
  single stray glow color, let's use either our house five or sampled depending on whether it
  "bleeds" from media." He answered house five for this beat, so it is the house five (no `colors`
  prop on the lamp).
- the shape, `item:bloom=keep`, on the card that read: "A one-shot that decays to a base, never to
  nothing, so the object stays lit afterwards. Lands: a resting base under the one-shot, on the
  publish beat and the QR plate. The plate already carries it." So a shared reel RESTS LIT: it looks
  different from a draft for as long as it is shared, and goes dark when the host unshares.
- never on a light ground: "No light ground usage is a decision for now." The studio is always a dark
  room; the share card follows the app's theme, so in light mode it takes NO coloured light and the
  state change (the check, "Shared with guests") carries the moment alone.
- the halo's fence, the same day: "Do not like as a button wrapper, only to light objects from
  behind." The Share BUTTON never wears a glow; the lit object is the reel's frame, or the card.
- the placement law: "custom and bespoke, not a couple of identity components reused everywhere in the
  same way constantly." Compose each of the two for its place; they need not match each other.

## Questions (what the goal leaves open; a recommended answer each; the Orchestrator relays them and quotes the answer back)

All four answered by the Orchestrator in chat (2026-09-17): "Your recommendations on all four Questions
are accepted (the swell stays on the engine's clock; the rest-on-open behaviour stays; the opaque
`bg-card` share card stays; I withdraw the `publish-violet` ask and fix the stale `--tune-rxp-pub-ms`
comment at the merge, both outside your lane)."

- **The swell's clock.** The goal asked that `--tune-rxp-pub-ms` time the swell "if the engine's
  duration can read it without editing the engine". It cannot: the bloom's animation is a literal
  1400ms in the engine's shorthand and the engine exposes no duration for a one-shot (`--glw-dur` is
  read by the looping drives only). The only way in from outside is a rule on `[data-glw-band]` from the
  publish block, a second writer on the engine's own element. **Recommended and accepted: the swell
  stays on the engine's clock**, the same one-shot the QR plate runs ("consistent with the rest of our
  glows"); the knob still times the Studio's confirmation card (700 + 900 = 1600ms, a breath after the
  light settles). A tunable swell is one variable, `--glw-bloom-dur`, in an engine lane.
- **An already-shared reel does not open on a still.** The goal proposed holding `--glw-strength` at 0
  so "the base alone says this one is live". Built, and it does gate the swell, but it is not a still:
  `glw-bloom`'s last keyframe is `opacity: var(--glw-base)` whatever the strength, so the armed band
  eases from nothing up to the base over the same 1400ms and stops. Both roads therefore REST at the
  same level (base under band-at-base) and only the tap overshoots on the way. **Recommended and
  accepted: keep.** A true still needs the engine to accept a bloom "born spent", an engine lane's call.
- **The share card is opaque now.** It was a bare outline (`border`, no fill), and an outline cannot be
  lit from behind: the light shows through it, under the words, as a fill. It takes `bg-card` in both
  states: no visible change on a light ground (`--card` is the page's own value there), the raised
  Graphite surface on dark, the recipe `host-upload.tsx`'s card already wears on the same page.
  **Recommended and accepted: keep.**
- **Two stale lines outside this lane.** `glow-moments/spec.ts`'s open ask `publish-violet` is answered
  by the ruling and this wiring, and moment 07's lede and verdict label still record the old argument
  (left alone per the brief: only the "Today" specimen changed). `globals.css`'s `:root` line
  `--tune-rxp-pub-ms: 700ms; /* publish flourish (share-card bloom / studio glow) */` is outside the
  release and its comment is stale: no CSS reads the knob any more. Proposed text: `/* the publish
  beat: the Studio's confirmation card's hold (read by JS only) */`. **Accepted: the Orchestrator
  withdraws the ask and fixes the comment at the merge.**
