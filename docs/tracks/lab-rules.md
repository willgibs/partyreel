# lp/lab-rules

**Goal.** The rule layer: everything that influences design work, visible and levelled. (1) The authority
model: `rules/influences.ts` exports `LEVELS` (law, contract, policy, program, guidance, precedent,
proposal, ruling, landmine: the badge and one line each; `docs/design/README.md#what-binds-you` is the
only place the rule is written) and the influences registry (`Influence {id, title, level, scope:
global|surface|component|board|wave, source: ts|test|md|skill, author: Will|agent|third-party, summary,
visibleAt, ruledOn?, enforces?}`): law from `BIBLE`; contracts and policies from the artifact; board
rulings from `RULINGS` and `design-record.md`; rulings, guidance, program principles, doctrine sections
and landmines indexed from markdown at request time through `_data/docs.ts`. `bindsFor({board,
surface, ownedPaths})` returns the Binds strip for a board or a track: the law (the rules `under
exploration: <this board>` marked as yours to rewrite), the contracts of owned components, the policies
in scope, the program rules, the surface's landmines. (2) The collector v3 (`scripts/design-rules/`):
a `// @policy: global|marketing|guest|host|shared|lab|engineering` directive in the same header position
as `@contract-for` (a file may carry both); the artifact gains `policies[] {file, scope, title, summary,
line}`; a directive found past the 40-line window throws with the path (`sandbox/media-kit/plan.test.ts`
is the case that proves it); the fourteen policy tests you own gain their line; `rules.ts` exports
`POLICIES`; the freshness guard compares them like contracts; a test refuses a design policy no bible
rule cites and a `*-policy.test.ts` without a directive. (3) The library as a file:
`docs/design/library.md` generated by `pnpm design:rules` beside the artifact (the levels and what binds
you, the 22 rules with why and status, the policies by scope with what each refuses, the guidance
pointers, the component index with each `for` line and contract titles, the standing boards with their
open asks), freshness-guarded by the same test. (4) The pages, on the shell templates: `rules` (the
eight groups; `[id]` permalinks with why, enforced-by through `Ref`, status linking the board, the
proposal it inherits, prev/next in bible order; the contract list leaves for the component pages),
`policies` (`#tests` from `POLICIES`, `#landmines` from the two system docs), `guidance` (renders
`docs/design/guidance.md`: the craft stack moved out of `design-system.md` with a pointer left, plus a
Skills table: `emil-design-eng` primary, `transitions-polish` the motion audit of a wiring round,
`transitions-dev` recipes, the user-global ones marked outside the repo; a test refuses a skill
directory nobody names), `rulings`, `doctrine/[doc]` (each with its `Callout`), `record` (Open and
Ruled rows with every `lives` path a `Ref`; `[id]` renders the record section with the board, rule,
proposal and track links; the record's hand table is deleted and derived from `RULINGS`; `event-feed`
gets its entry or goes, forced by a test), `glossary`. (5) The review ledger reader (`review/`: the zod
schema `{board, rounds[{n, opened, answers[{ask, choice, note?, by, at}], notes[]}]}`, `readLedger(board)`,
`status.ts` deriving answered vs open from spec minus ledger; a test checks every answer against the
spec in `sandbox/registry.ts`); the Orchestrator writes the ledgers, you read them. (6) A health strip
on the rules page computed from the registry: components without a contract, rules enforced by review
only, design policies no rule cites, contracts whose test moved, specs whose board has landed. (7) The
docs diet in lane, subtractively: sentences in the two system docs that restate bible rules become
"bible N" pointers; `marketing-content.md`'s grounds and voice paragraphs point at bible 16 and 20/21;
the specs' "asks, one word each" blocks are deleted (the spec is one home). Phase 0 left stubs of every
page you own; replace them, keep their routes.

**Binds.** The bible, the contracts of every component under a path you own, and the policies
(`/design/library/policies`); everything else is precedent (`docs/design/README.md#what-binds-you`,
rendered at `/design/library`). Shell changes outside your lane are asked for in the Handoff and the
Orchestrator lands them (announced in `docs/tracks/orchestrator.md`); never edit another track's
files, `touchpoints.ts`, `rules/bible.ts`, CHANGELOG, STATUS, ROADMAP, PROGRAM, CLAUDE, AGENTS,
`docs/ASSETS.md`, `docs/design/rulings.md` or `docs/reviews/`. Light QA (Will, 2026-09-14): your pages
at 1440 and 375 in a foreground tab, light and dark, reduced motion honoured, the gate green on the
synced tree (`pnpm typecheck && pnpm lint && pnpm test && pnpm build`, then `pnpm lab:smoke` against
`pnpm dev`). Push freely (no CI on `lp/*` unless the message says `[ci]`); the preview builds at
`status: handed-off` or on `[preview]`. No em-dashes anywhere a person reads.

**The round.** The Library x Lab round (Will, 2026-09-15): "we need a dedicated round of library and
lab UI work to make nav and presentation better before I can review the track work itself... Think of
this as building our own internal app to manage our design system, exploratory lab work that gets
merged in... the goal is for the library to represent our entire working rule set so that everything
influencing new agents' design work is visible to both me as a human, you as an orchestrator, and new
agents." shadcn's docs site (https://ui.shadcn.com/docs/components/base/attachment) is the layout
reference: top nav, a left sidebar of sections and lists, a content column with title, description,
previews with code, prev/next, a right-hand table of contents. The plan's whole text is
`docs/design/rulings.md` (2026-09-15) plus this manifest; the shell exists (Phase 0, on
`launch-prep`): read `src/app/(dev)/design/(shell)/_shell/index.ts` and `_data/catalog.ts` first,
then walk `/design/library` and `/design/lab` on `pnpm dev` before writing a line.

**Rulings in force.** Copy-as-message only: the review panel composes a message Will pastes into
chat; the UI never writes the repo. Will's rulings live in `docs/design/rulings.md` (never owned).
Routes are `/design/library/*` and `/design/lab/*`; `.mono` is retired (one design language, the real
tokens). This round builds the shell, the kit, the rules layer and the desk; the boards migrate in
the wave after it.

**Verify on.** `/design/library/rules` renders the levels with badges, the 22 laws, the policies grouped by scope
with the bible ids they enforce, no contracts; `/design/library/policies` lists every `@policy:` test
with its line; `/design/library/glow` shows only its contracts (the library track's page reads your
`bindsFor`); a board's Binds strip names the rule that is its own to rewrite; the late-directive throw
proven; `/design/library/record` derives its table from `RULINGS` and renders every section;
`docs/design/library.md` regenerates with the artifact and its freshness test fails when a rule,
policy, contract or `for` line changes without it. The gate and `pnpm lab:smoke` green.
