deleted or bent to fit. It stays visible, badged, with an agent's one-line opinion and a way for him to say "the
ruling stands". A picked option should not be allowed to kill a better idea it never met. That became the
`overtaken` lane.

and the YAML parser refused every one; a bare token and a comment fixed it. The ladder lane's file list overlapped
the Orchestrator's own files and the dev tuners; the manifest guard caught it before a lane was cut, which is the
guard doing its job. The bindsFor marker test needed a bible rule under exploration to prove itself, and bible 20
had just been ruled; it now waits for the next such rule instead of failing.
