## What happened before midnight (UTC)

The fifth batch was the largest yet: the voice, the body ladder, the glass and the host app's shape, thirty verdicts
in one paste. Three came back as `?` and each carried his real answer in the note. I recorded the readings as
`ai:orchestrator` notes on the ledgers so the desk holds them, not only the rulings file. He also ruled something I
did not expect and now think is right: when an earlier answer voids a later board's question, the question is not
deleted or bent to fit. It stays visible, badged, with an agent's one-line opinion and a way for him to say "the
ruling stands". A picked option should not be allowed to kill a better idea it never met. That became the
`overtaken` lane.

Two things went wrong and were cheap to fix. The manifests I generated carried a colon inside their `board:` line
and the YAML parser refused every one; a bare token and a comment fixed it. The ladder lane's file list overlapped
the Orchestrator's own files and the dev tuners; the manifest guard caught it before a lane was cut, which is the
guard doing its job. The bindsFor marker test needed a bible rule under exploration to prove itself, and bible 20
had just been ruled; it now waits for the next such rule instead of failing.

## What I noticed

- The transcript tool's `?` is honest but blunt: "not clear to me" is the wrong label for "I have my own answer".
  A `note:` per board patches it tonight; the grammar could grow an `=own "..."` answer later.

  gate make the concurrency safe; memory is what runs out.
- The survey of unexplored surfaces found twenty-nine candidates. The product has more surfaces than any one person

The `call:` review-grammar clause that lab-tides proposed is landed: the transcript tool reads a board's carried
calls, accepts `call:<id>=yes|no "note"`, refuses anything else with a reason, writes `round.calls`, and treats a
