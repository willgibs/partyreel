A shared album is a lovely thing to have. But let's be honest about what people actually rewatch: not 400 photos, but the one short video that strings the night's best moments together. That's the highlight reel, and it's the feature Partyreel is named for.

## From a pile of clips to one video worth keeping

By the end of an event you've got hundreds of photos and dozens of clips spread across everyone's phones. Sorting through all of it to make something shareable is exactly the kind of work that never happens.

So we do it for you. Partyreel pulls the standout moments from everything your guests captured and compiles them into one short, polished reel: no editor, no timeline, no afternoon lost to it.

<Callout type="tip" title="The morning-after test">

The album is what you browse once. The reel is what you send to the group the next morning and watch three more times yourself.

</Callout>

## It's automatic, but it's yours

The first cut is made for you, but you have the final say. Keep the automatic selection, or step in and choose exactly which moments make it in and which sit out. It's your event, and the reel should feel like it.

## Easy to share, yours to keep

When it's ready, the reel is as simple to pass around as anything else in Partyreel:

- **Share** it with a link so guests can watch without digging through the album.
- **Download** it as a finished video to keep or post wherever you like.

And the individual photos and videos always stay [downloadable on their own](/help/download-photos-videos-and-albums); the reel is a bonus on top, not a replacement.
