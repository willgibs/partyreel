A clip you save is yours alone. Adding it to the event gives everyone a copy: it joins the album as a video, next to the photos it was made from.

## Add it

Make your clip first (see [make your own clip](/help/make-your-own-clip)). On the finished clip, tap <UiLabel>Add to event</UiLabel>, then confirm. The confirm names the event and says what happens next, ending on <UiLabel>The live reel won't play it.</UiLabel>, so a stray tap never adds anything. You stay on the finished clip afterward, where the button reads <UiLabel>Added</UiLabel>, so you can still share or save it.

## Where it goes

- **The host's clip** goes into the album approved, straight away, and uses a little of your storage, like any video you upload; the confirm says about how much. See [storage, plans, and what counts toward your limit](/help/storage-plans-and-limits).
- **A guest's clip** goes in like any guest upload, through the same queue as your photos. On an event with review turned on, the host approves it first, and the confirm says so; see [review uploads before they appear](/help/review-uploads-before-they-appear).

Once it's in, it's an ordinary video: everyone who can open the album can watch it, save it, and download it with the rest, and the host can hide or remove it like anything else.

## What it never does

The highlight reel never plays a clip back, so the reel doesn't end up showing itself. A clip in the album also doesn't count toward starting the reel.

## When it can't go in

- **On a free event.** A clip is a video, and videos need <PlanBadge tier="paid" />. Share it or save it instead.
- **While uploads are closed.** Guests can't add a clip then; the host still can.
- **When the album is full.** Like any upload, it can't go in until the host frees up space.
- **After a lot of clips in one day.** A guest who adds many in a day sees <UiLabel>You've added a lot of clips today. Try again tomorrow.</UiLabel>

Something else in the way? See [a clip won't finish or save](/help/a-clip-wont-finish-or-save).
