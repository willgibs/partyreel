This switch decides when the album opens for a guest: right after they give a name or confirm an email, or only once they've added something themselves.

<Path>Dashboard › Your event › Settings › Guest uploads › Require an upload to view</Path>

## Off (the default)

A guest who joins sees the full album as soon as they've given a name, or confirmed their email when [Require verified emails](/help/require-verified-emails-explained) is on. There's no extra step: once they're past the door, everything you and your other guests have shared is right there.

## On

Flip the switch on and Partyreel asks you to confirm with <UiLabel>Ask for a photo before the album?</UiLabel>. It spells out the trade: guests will see a preview of <TeaserCount /> photos and add one of their own before the album opens, and if uploads are closed or the album has hit its cap, it opens anyway rather than holding anyone at a step they can't finish. <UiLabel>Leave it open</UiLabel> backs out; <UiLabel>Ask for a photo</UiLabel> goes ahead, then tap <UiLabel>Save changes</UiLabel>. Turning it back off is instant and needs no confirmation.

A guest reaches this step the same way they always would, by giving a name or confirming an email first; this switch only adds what happens next. Once they've added a single photo or video, whatever happens to it afterward (you hide it, they remove it), they're in for good: nobody is asked twice.

## Which to choose

- **On** when you want every guest holding a phone to actually use it: a wedding or a big party where "I'll upload it later" usually means never.
- **Off** for a quieter event, or one where some guests are only there to look: grandparents at a birthday, a work event with a mix of contributors and spectators.

Settings shows one live sentence describing exactly what a guest will experience with your current combination, right where you set it.

<Callout type="info" title="This isn't a password">

Requiring an upload doesn't stop anyone with the link from joining, on or off; it only changes when they see everything. To limit who can get in at all, add a password: see [password-protect your event](/help/password-protect-your-event).

</Callout>
