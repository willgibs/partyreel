Running out of room is undramatic. The album keeps working for everyone; only new uploads wait until there's space again.

## At the limit

Guests who try to add a photo see <UiLabel>This album is full right now. The host needs to free up space.</UiLabel> Your own uploads get <UiLabel>Storage is full for your plan. Free up space or upgrade.</UiLabel> Everything already in the album stays exactly as it is: viewing, the reel, downloads, sharing, all unaffected. The bell on your dashboard flags it, and the storage meter goes red.

Two ways out, both instant:

- **Delete.** Remove photos you don't want, or a whole event. Space comes back the moment you do; you don't wait for Deleted to clear. Hidden photos still count, so hiding isn't enough.
- **Upgrade.** Move up a plan or a Pro size from the [pricing page](/pricing); uploads resume as soon as the payment goes through. See [upgrade, downgrade, or cancel](/help/upgrade-downgrade-or-cancel).

Restoring from Deleted needs room too: if you're full, <UiLabel>Restore</UiLabel> refuses and offers an upgrade.

## A monthly upload limit

Separately from storage, each plan has a generous monthly allowance for how much can be uploaded, a guard against churn rather than a number you'll meet at a real event. If you did, guests would see <UiLabel>This album has hit its upload limit for the month.</UiLabel> and it clears with the calendar.

## After a downgrade

If you cancel Pro, or an Event Pass ends, and your albums hold more than the new plan allows, nothing is deleted on the spot. You get <OverCapGraceDays /> of full access, with an email at the start and a reminder near the end, to make room or move back up. Only if that window passes with nothing changed does Partyreel trim the largest files first into Deleted, where they wait the usual <RecoveryDays /> and can still be restored once there's room. Uploads are paused throughout, since you're over the limit.

<Callout type="tip" title="The quickest space">

Videos are the big files. If you're a few hundred megabytes short, one long clip in Select mode is usually the whole fix.

</Callout>
