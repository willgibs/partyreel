Every Partyreel account has three ways in: a password, a code by email, and Google (see [sign in: password, email code, or Google](/help/sign-in-options-and-passwords)). If one won't open, another almost always will, and they all lead to the same account when the email matches.

## The password doesn't work

The message reads <UiLabel>That email and password didn't match. If you usually sign in with Google or an email code, use one of those below, or reset your password.</UiLabel> It's deliberately vague: it won't tell you whether the email is unknown, the password is wrong, or the account never had a password (accounts created with a code or Google don't, until you add one).

Do what it says: tap <UiLabel>Email me a code instead</UiLabel>, or <UiLabel>Continue with Google</UiLabel>. Once you're in, the <UiLabel>Password</UiLabel> card on your account page sets a new one; or use <UiLabel>Forgot password?</UiLabel> on the sign-in page, which emails a code and then lets you pick a new password.

## The sign-in link expired

Tapping an old link from an earlier email shows <UiLabel>That sign-in link didn't work. It may have expired. Try again below.</UiLabel> Request a fresh code; only the newest one is valid.

## The code never arrives

See [the email code didn't arrive](/help/the-email-code-didnt-arrive).

## "My events are gone"

You're almost certainly in a different account. Partyreel matches accounts by email, exactly: `you@gmail.com` and `you+party@gmail.com` are two accounts, and so are a work and a personal address. Sign out, sign back in with the address you used when you created the event, and it's there. Google sign-in uses the address on the Google account you pick.

## Two accounts by accident

If you've ended up with events split across two addresses, [send us a note](/contact) from both and say which one you'd like to keep; we'll sort it out. There's no self-serve merge yet.

<Callout type="info" title="Signed out unexpectedly?">

Sessions do expire after a long while, and signing out on the event page also clears your guest access to that event. Sign back in and you're where you were.

</Callout>
