The heart never shows a number to the room. It tells the host which photos landed, and it's the first thing the highlight reel looks at.

## Liking

Tap the heart on a tile (on a desktop, it appears on hover) or in the photo viewer. Tap again to unlike. Guests who aren't signed in are asked to confirm an email first (the same free account as everything else), because a like is tied to a person.

Every photo you've liked, on any event, collects under <UiLabel>Likes</UiLabel> on your dashboard, newest first. Unliking there drops it from the list.

## What the host sees

On the host's view of the gallery, a photo with likes carries a small badge with the count. That badge is host-only: guests never see totals, so nobody's photo gets publicly ranked. It's the room voting, privately, for the host's benefit.

## What likes feed

- **The reel's first cut.** When you start a reel, Partyreel offers a fill based on what the crowd liked (<UiLabel>Start with the crowd favorites</UiLabel>) once enough likes exist, blended with recent photos and a spread across contributors. Likes are a signal, not a rule: a photo in the reel is there because you kept it, and a liked photo isn't in the reel until you add it. See [the highlight reel](/help/the-highlight-reel).
- **Your own shortlist.** Liking as you scroll is a fast way to mark keepers, and <UiLabel>Select</UiLabel> then <UiLabel>Like</UiLabel> marks a batch at once.

## What they don't do

There's no "sort by likes" on the album yet, for hosts or guests; the gallery is always newest first. Likes don't affect what's shown, hidden, or approved, and they aren't visible on a public profile.

<Callout type="tip" title="Tell the room to heart things">

A line in the announcement ("heart your favorites and we'll cut the reel from them") turns the heart into the crowd's vote, which makes a better reel and a better story the morning after.

</Callout>
