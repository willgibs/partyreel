Review is the switch between "the album fills live" and "nothing appears until I've seen it". Both are right for different events, and you can flip it at any point.

## Turn it on

<Path>Dashboard › Your event › Settings › Guest uploads</Path>

Flip <UiLabel>Review uploads before they appear</UiLabel> and tap <UiLabel>Save changes</UiLabel>. Or open <UiLabel>Review</UiLabel> from your event page, where <UiLabel>Turn on review</UiLabel> does it in one tap.

From then on, a guest's upload goes into Review instead of the gallery. The guest sees a note that the host reviews uploads first, and their own uploads list says <UiLabel>Waiting for approval</UiLabel> until you decide. Your own uploads never wait; the host is the reviewer.

## Approving and rejecting

<Path>Dashboard › Your event › Review</Path>

While anything is waiting, the <UiLabel>Review</UiLabel> card on your event page shows how many, and the bell on your dashboard says it too. Inside, every upload sits in a box of the same size, so a long queue is quick to scan. Three ways through it:

- <UiLabel>Approve all</UiLabel>: the fast path. Most review is a quick scroll, then this.
- Open one: tap an upload to see it large, with <UiLabel>Reject</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>Approve</UiLabel> under it. Decide, and the next one opens.
- <UiLabel>Select</UiLabel>: tap the ones you mean, then <UiLabel>Approve</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>Reject</UiLabel> the selection. <UiLabel>All</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>Clear</UiLabel> speed it up.

Every approve or reject names itself on a toast with <UiLabel>Undo</UiLabel> for a few seconds, and Undo puts those uploads back in the queue exactly where they were.

Approved photos appear for guests within seconds. When the queue empties you get a small "all caught up" note. Anything you reject lands in your album hidden: dimmed for you, where <UiLabel>Show</UiLabel> approves it after all, and never shown to guests. The guest who sent it sees <UiLabel>Not approved</UiLabel> beside it in their own uploads; nobody else sees any sign of it. See [hide, remove, and restore](/help/hide-remove-and-restore).

Not sure about one? Review says it above the queue: <UiLabel>Anything you approve can still be hidden later.</UiLabel> Hiding takes a photo off every guest's album at once, so approving and hiding later is always open to you.

## On a keyboard

At a computer the queue takes the keys you'd expect. The arrow keys move between uploads, <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> approves the one you're on and <Kbd>Backspace</Kbd> rejects it, moving you on to the next. <Kbd>Space</Kbd> opens it large, where the same keys decide it and the arrows step through the queue; <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> closes it.

## New uploads while you're reviewing

An upload that arrives while you're working through Review never slips into the queue under you. A line above the queue says how many are new, and tapping it adds them to the top. That way nothing moves while you have a selection going, and the queue never says you're caught up while new ones wait.

## Turning it off

Flip the switch off and save. If uploads are waiting, Partyreel asks first: <UiLabel>Stop reviewing uploads?</UiLabel> explains that turning it off approves everything in the queue and shows it to everyone right away. <UiLabel>Keep reviewing</UiLabel> backs out; <UiLabel>Approve all and stop</UiLabel> (or <UiLabel>Approve it and stop</UiLabel> for a single upload) goes ahead. Turning review off always releases the queue; there's no way to switch it off and keep photos waiting.

## It's never retroactive

Turning review on doesn't pull already-approved photos back into the queue; they stay live. Turning it off doesn't hide anything. The switch only decides what happens to the next upload.

## When to use it

- **On**: weddings, anything on a screen in the room, corporate events, albums a client or a family elder will see, and any event where you'd rather look first.
- **Off**: parties, trips, reunions, and anywhere the live fill is the fun. You can still hide or remove afterward.

<Callout type="info" title="A photo that's missing">

If a guest says their photo isn't showing, Review is the first place to look. The other causes are in [a photo is missing from the album](/help/a-photo-is-missing-from-the-album).

</Callout>
