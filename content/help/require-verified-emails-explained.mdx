This one switch decides whether an upload's name is confirmed. On, every guest confirms an email before they're in. Off, a guest types a display name instead, and it rides on their photos wearing a small unverified mark.

<Path>Dashboard › Your event › Settings › Guest uploads › Require verified emails</Path>

## On (the default)

A guest who opens the link sees the newest <TeaserCount /> photos as a preview, with a count of the rest. To see everything and add their own, they confirm an email: a 6-digit code (or a link) in one email, no password, no app. That confirmation is a free Partyreel account, which is also how they can save the event and find their uploads later. Then they pick a display name.

For you, that means every upload in the album carries a confirmed name, and the email behind it shows in your own view of the photo. It's on for every plan at no cost.

## What you can see, and can't do

The uploader's email appears only to you, per photo, in the photo viewer on your event page. Guests never see it, and it isn't listed anywhere else: there's no guest email export and no way to message your guests through Partyreel. It's there so you know who sent what, not as a mailing list.

## Off

Flip the switch off and Partyreel asks you to confirm with <UiLabel>Let guests upload without verifying?</UiLabel>. It spells out the trade: guests type a display name instead of confirming an email, their photos carry a small unverified mark, abuse is harder to trace, and you won't capture their email. <UiLabel>Keep emails required</UiLabel> backs out; <UiLabel>Allow unverified uploads</UiLabel> goes ahead, then tap <UiLabel>Save changes</UiLabel>. Turning it back on is instant and needs no confirmation.

A guest who types a name can confirm an email later and clear the mark; [save an event, and find your uploads later](/help/save-an-event-and-find-your-uploads) covers that side of it.

## Which to choose

- **On** for almost everything: weddings, family events, anything with children in the photos, corporate events, and any event with a screen in the room.
- **Off** for a small, trusted crowd where the extra step would cost you photos, or an event where guests have explicitly asked not to give an email.

On, a guest who'd rather not confirm can still browse the preview; only the full album and uploading need the email. Off, there's no preview to weigh: typing a name is all it takes to see everything and add right away. Guests' own side of it is in [why an event asks for your email](/help/why-an-event-asks-for-your-email).

<Callout type="info" title="This isn't a password">

Requiring verified emails confirms who a guest is; it doesn't stop anyone with the link from joining, on or off. To limit who can get in at all, add a password: see [password-protect your event](/help/password-protect-your-event).

</Callout>
