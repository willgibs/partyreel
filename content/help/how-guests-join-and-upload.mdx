The whole point of Partyreel is that guests don't have to work for it. There's no app to download and no account to create — if they can scan a QR code, they can contribute.

## What a guest does

1. **Scan the QR code** with their phone camera (or tap the join link).
2. **Enter a display name** so their uploads are recognizable. That's the only thing they're asked for — unless you've chosen to request an email too.
3. **Upload** photos and videos right from the browser. They can keep adding all night.

<Callout type="tip" title="Uploads appear live">

When your event is public, new photos show up in the gallery as guests add them — so everyone can watch the album fill in real time.

</Callout>

## What guests can upload

- **Photos** up to <PhotoSize /> each — plenty of room for high-resolution shots and iPhone HEICs.
- **Videos** up to <VideoMinutes /> minutes and <VideoSize /> — long enough for speeches, toasts, and first dances.

Everything uploads at full quality, straight from the phone — no messaging-app compression.

## Do guests need an account?

No. A guest only enters a display name, plus an email if you've turned that on for your event. There's no sign-up, no password, and nothing installed. If you're the host, see [who can see your event](/help/who-can-see-your-event) to decide how open it is.
