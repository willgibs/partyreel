The whole point of Partyreel is that guests don't have to work for it. There's no app to download and no password to invent: if they can scan a QR code, they can contribute.

## What a guest does

1. **Scan the QR code** with their phone camera (or tap the join link).
2. **Confirm their email with a one-time code**, if your event asks for one. That single tap doubles as their account; there's no form and no password.
3. **Upload** right from the browser. They can keep adding as the event goes on, and new uploads appear for everyone.

<Callout type="tip" title="Uploads appear live">

When your event is public, new photos show up in the gallery as guests add them, so everyone can watch the album fill in real time.

</Callout>

## What guests can upload

- **Photos** up to <UploadSize /> each: plenty of room for high-resolution shots and iPhone HEICs.
- **Videos**, on events hosted with Pro or an Event Pass, up to the same <UploadSize /> ceiling: long enough for speeches, toasts, and first dances. Free events collect photos only.

Everything uploads at full quality, straight from the phone, with no messaging-app compression. Before anything leaves the phone, Partyreel also strips location (GPS) and camera metadata in the browser; see [who can see your event](/help/who-can-see-your-event).

## Do guests need an account?

Never an app, and never a password. Whether they verify an email is your call as the host:

- **"Require accounts" on** (the default for new events): a guest confirms their email with a one-time code before seeing the full album and uploading. Until then, a visitor sees just a preview of the newest photos. The code confirmation is the whole sign-up, and it means every upload is attributed to a real, verified guest.
- **"Require accounts" off**: guests view and upload with no account at all. Their uploads show as "Anonymous" unless they later sign in.

Signed-in guests pick a display name once, so their uploads carry their name in the album. If you're the host, see [who can see your event](/help/who-can-see-your-event) to decide how open it is.
