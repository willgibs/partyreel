Nothing about a clip happens on a server: your own phone or laptop draws it, frame by frame, and hands you the file. That makes it free and private, and it means the few things that can stop it are all on the device in your hand.

## Make your own is greyed out

Tap it anyway and it says why: this browser can't make clips. The reel still plays. Open the album on another device, or in the current version of Safari, Chrome, Edge, or Firefox; an in-app browser (a link opened inside a messaging or social app) is the usual culprit, so open the page in the real browser instead.

## It started, then stopped

Making a clip pauses while the tab is in the background and picks up when you come back, so keep it open and in front until it's done. If a clip doesn't finish, you land back on it with a way to try again and your picks kept.

## Share didn't open anything

Some browsers can't hand a video to the share sheet. Save the clip instead and share it from your camera roll or files. Dismissing the share sheet on purpose is fine: nothing is lost, and the clip is still there to share or save.

## I can't find the saved file

On an iPhone, choosing Photos puts it in your camera roll; choosing Files, or downloading on any other device, puts it in your downloads. Search for the event's name: the file is named after it.

## Add to event isn't there, or didn't work

Adding a clip needs a paid event and open uploads, and a guest who adds a lot of clips in one day is asked to wait until tomorrow. On an event with review, a guest's clip waits for the host before it shows. See [add a clip to the event](/help/add-a-clip-to-the-event).

<Callout type="info" title="Your picks aren't stored either">

Partyreel never keeps a copy of your clip or the picks you made. Save the file once it's done if you want it later; the reel is always there to make another.

</Callout>
