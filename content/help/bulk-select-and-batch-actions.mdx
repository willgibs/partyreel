Curating one photo at a time is fine for one photo. For the forty near-duplicates from the cake table, there's Select mode.

## Enter Select mode

<Path>Dashboard › Your event › Gallery › Select</Path>

Tap <UiLabel>Select</UiLabel> in the gallery header, or press and hold any tile for about half a second (that tile becomes your first pick). Tiles turn into checkboxes: tap to pick, tap again to unpick. The bar at the bottom shows your count with <UiLabel>All</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>Clear</UiLabel> beside it.

## The actions

- <UiLabel>Like</UiLabel>: hearts them all.
- <UiLabel>Hide</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>Show</UiLabel>: the label flips to Show only when everything you've picked is already hidden.
- <UiLabel>Download</UiLabel>: bundles just the selection into a zip, no dialog, hidden ones included.
- <UiLabel>Delete</UiLabel>: asks you to confirm (the dialog names the count), then removes them all to Deleted.
- The X (<UiLabel>Cancel selection</UiLabel>) leaves Select mode.

Like reports once for the batch ("Liked 12 photos") rather than once per photo, and anything you'd already liked is skipped without a message. There's no reel action here: the highlight reel plays every photo guests can see on its own, so hiding a photo is how you keep it out. See [the highlight reel](/help/the-highlight-reel).

## In Review

The Review section has its own Select mode with a smaller bar: <UiLabel>Approve</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>Hide</UiLabel> for the picked uploads. See [review uploads before they appear](/help/review-uploads-before-they-appear).

## Your selection survives

If new uploads arrive while you're mid-selection, the album updates underneath you and your picks stay picked. Only leaving Select mode clears them.

<Callout type="tip" title="The morning-after pass">

Open the event on a laptop, tap <UiLabel>Select</UiLabel>, walk the album once hiding the misfires, then <UiLabel>All</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>Download</UiLabel> for a full copy. Ten minutes for a whole wedding.

</Callout>
