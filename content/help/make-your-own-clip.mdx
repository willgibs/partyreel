The reel plays on; a clip is the piece of it you keep. It's a short video you make from the album on your own phone or laptop, saved or shared as a file. Anyone who can open the whole album can make one, the host included, on every plan.

## Start one

Open the reel from the <UiLabel>Highlight reel</UiLabel> tile at the top of the album, then tap <UiLabel>Make your own</UiLabel>, the violet button under the reel's controls. The line under the tile's heading, <UiLabel>Make your own clip to share</UiLabel>, opens it directly.

Your clip starts out already made: the reel's own picks, fitted to the length, in the look you were watching. Everything from here is optional.

## Shape it

Two tabs hold the choices, one open at a time:

- <UiLabel>Looks</UiLabel> shows every look drawn on your own clip. Tap one and the clip changes while it plays.
- <UiLabel>Moments</UiLabel> is the album. The numbered photos are in your clip, in order; tap one to take it out, or any other to add it. The chips swap the whole set: <UiLabel>The reel's picks</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Only mine</UiLabel>, or <UiLabel>Everything</UiLabel>.

The order strip is the running order (under the clip at a laptop, inside Moments on a phone): <UiLabel>Hold a moment to move it. The clip keeps playing.</UiLabel> The tray at the foot holds <UiLabel>Length</UiLabel> (<UiLabel>Auto</UiLabel> fits the clip to your moments, up to your event's longest: <ReelSeconds tier="free" /> seconds on a free event, <ReelSeconds tier="pro" /> on a paid one), <UiLabel>Layout</UiLabel> (portrait for phones and Stories, landscape for a TV), and <UiLabel>Opening</UiLabel>, the moment that starts it. More on each in [looks, length, and layout](/help/reel-styles-length-and-layout).

Guests never see a hidden photo here. The host sees hers marked <UiLabel>Hidden · Show</UiLabel>; Show puts it back in the album for everyone and into the clip.

## Make it

Tap <UiLabel>Make it</UiLabel> and your device draws the clip frame by frame, counting down the moments left: <UiLabel>Drawing your clip on this device. Keep this tab open.</UiLabel> It pauses while the tab is in the background and picks up when you come back. <UiLabel>Cancel</UiLabel> stops it and keeps your picks.

## Keep it

When it's done, <UiLabel>Your clip is ready</UiLabel>, and you stay on it:

- <UiLabel>Share</UiLabel> opens your device's share sheet: Messages, Instagram, anywhere.
- <UiLabel>Save</UiLabel> keeps the file. On an iPhone you choose <UiLabel>Save to Photos</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>Download file</UiLabel>; elsewhere it downloads.
- <UiLabel>Add to event</UiLabel>, on a paid event, puts a copy in the album. See [add a clip to the event](/help/add-a-clip-to-the-event).

Share, save, and add in any order; each one says when it's done. Nothing about your clip is kept for you, so save it if you want it later. <UiLabel>Make another</UiLabel> starts a fresh one from the reel's next picks, in the same look, length, and layout.

## The fine print

Clips are silent, so add a song in the app you post from. A free event's clips carry a small partyreel.com mark in the corner, and one quiet line under the clip says so (<UiLabel>Free events mark their clips.</UiLabel>); paid events' clips don't. Something not working? See [a clip won't finish or save](/help/a-clip-wont-finish-or-save).
