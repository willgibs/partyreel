The reel plays on; a clip is the piece of it you keep. It's a short video you make from the album on your own phone or laptop, saved or shared as a file. Anyone who can open the whole album can make one, the host included, on every plan.

## Start one

Open the reel from the <UiLabel>Highlight reel</UiLabel> tile at the top of the album, then tap <UiLabel>Make your own</UiLabel>, the violet button under the reel's controls. The line under the tile's heading, <UiLabel>Make your own clip to share</UiLabel>, opens it too.

Your clip starts out already made: the reel's own picks, cut to fit, in the reel's look. Everything from here is optional.

## Shape it

- **Looks** shows every look as your own clip's opening frame. Tap one and the clip changes while it plays.
- **Moments** is the album. The numbered photos are in your clip, in order; tap one to take it out, or any other to add it. "Start from" swaps the whole set: the reel's picks, only what you added, or everything.
- **The strip** under the clip is the running order. Hold a moment to move it; the clip keeps playing while you do.
- **Length** runs from Auto (fitted to your moments) up to your event's longest: <ReelSeconds tier="free" /> seconds on a free event, <ReelSeconds tier="pro" /> on a paid one. **Layout** is portrait for phones and Stories, or landscape for a TV. **Opening** picks the first moment.

More on each in [looks, length, and layout](/help/reel-styles-length-and-layout). Guests never see hidden photos here; the host sees them marked, with a way to show one again.

## Make it

Tap "Make it" and your device draws the clip frame by frame, counting down the moments left. Keep the tab open and in front: it pauses in the background and picks up when you come back. Cancel stops it and keeps your picks.

## Keep it

When it's done, you stay on the finished clip:

- <UiLabel>Share</UiLabel> opens your device's share sheet: Messages, Instagram, anywhere.
- <UiLabel>Save</UiLabel> keeps the file. On an iPhone you choose Photos or Files; elsewhere it downloads.
- **Add to event**, on a paid event, puts a copy in the album. See [add a clip to the event](/help/add-a-clip-to-the-event).

Share, save, and add in any order. Nothing about your clip is kept for you, so save it if you want it later; making another starts from your picks.

## The fine print

Clips are silent, so add a song in the app you post from. A free event's clips carry a small partyreel.com mark in the corner, and the clip maker says so; paid events' clips don't. Something not working? See [a clip won't finish or save](/help/a-clip-wont-finish-or-save).
