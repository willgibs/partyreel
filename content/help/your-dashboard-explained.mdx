Your dashboard is the home screen for everything tied to your account, whether you host events or just join them. It's a single feed with chips across the top to narrow it.

<Path>Account menu › Dashboard</Path>

## The chips

- <UiLabel>All</UiLabel> stacks your events, uploads, and likes in one scroll.
- <UiLabel>Events</UiLabel> shows the events you host and the events you've saved as a guest, mixed together by recency. Hosted events carry a calendar icon; saved ones a bookmark.
- <UiLabel>Following</UiLabel> lists events published by hosts you follow. See [your public profile, following, and blocking](/help/your-public-profile-following-and-blocking).
- <UiLabel>Uploads</UiLabel> is every photo and video you've added to any event, yours or someone else's, newest first (the most recent 200).
- <UiLabel>Likes</UiLabel> is every photo you've tapped the heart on, across every event.
- <UiLabel>Deleted</UiLabel> holds events you've deleted, with a countdown until they clear for good. See [hide, remove, and restore](/help/hide-remove-and-restore).

## Event cards

Each hosted event shows its name, date, how many items are in the album (approved ones; anything waiting in Review isn't counted), and whether it's <UiLabel>Open</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>Closed</UiLabel> to new uploads. An amber chip in the corner tells you when uploads are waiting for review. The small QR chip on the card opens the share dialog without leaving the dashboard.

A saved event (one you joined) shows who's hosting it. If that host has since made it private, the card locks and stops linking.

## The storage meter

Above the feed, a slim bar shows how much of your plan's storage you've used. Tap it for the details: the exact numbers, how many photos or minutes of video the plan holds, and anything sitting in Deleted (it frees itself). Pass holders find <UiLabel>Renew Event Pass</UiLabel> here, subscribers <UiLabel>Manage billing</UiLabel>. That panel is the only billing page; there isn't a separate one. See [upgrade, downgrade, or cancel](/help/upgrade-downgrade-or-cancel).

The meter turns amber past 85 percent. What happens at 100 is in [what happens when storage fills up](/help/what-happens-when-storage-fills-up).

## The event limit

Under the heading, a line counts your events against your plan: the free plan holds <MaxEvents tier="free" />, each Event Pass adds one more, and Pro is unlimited. At the limit, <UiLabel>New event</UiLabel> is disabled and a note explains: delete an event to free the slot, or upgrade.

## The bell and the account menu

The bell collects the few things that need a decision; see [notifications and the emails Partyreel sends](/help/notifications-and-emails). Your avatar opens the account menu: <UiLabel>Account</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Back to site</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Help center</UiLabel>, the theme switch, and <UiLabel>Sign out</UiLabel>.

<Callout type="tip" title="Nothing here yet?">

A brand-new account sees an empty placeholder grid and a single button, <UiLabel>Create your first event</UiLabel>. The chips appear once there's something to filter.
