Your dashboard is the home screen for everything tied to your account, whether you host events or add photos to other people's. Top to bottom: what needs you, your storage, your events, and what just arrived.

Tap the Partyreel logo, top left, from anywhere in the app; on an album, the account menu has <UiLabel>Dashboard</UiLabel>.

## What needs you

Once you host an event, the top of the page names the one next thing each event wants, first match wins: uploads waiting for your review, uploads paused, an album with photos but no reel yet, an event happening tomorrow. Your storage joins them past 85 percent. Past three, the rest fold behind a chip that opens them in place. When nothing does, it says so: <UiLabel>Nothing needs you right now.</UiLabel>

## The storage meter

A slim bar shows how much of your plan's storage you've used. Tap it for the details: the exact numbers, how many photos or minutes of video the plan holds, and anything sitting in Deleted (it frees itself). <UiLabel>Need more?</UiLabel> opens the plans. Pass holders find <UiLabel>Renew Event Pass</UiLabel> here, subscribers <UiLabel>Manage billing</UiLabel>, which opens the billing portal for the card on file, invoices, and cancelling; a different plan size, or a switch between monthly and yearly, is picked in the plans instead. See [upgrade, downgrade, or cancel](/help/upgrade-downgrade-or-cancel).

The meter turns amber past 85 percent. What happens at 100 is in [what happens when storage fills up](/help/what-happens-when-storage-fills-up).

## The event limit

Under the heading, a line counts your events against your plan: the free plan holds <MaxEvents tier="free" />, each Event Pass adds one more, and Pro is unlimited. At the limit, <UiLabel>New event</UiLabel> still opens, and says what's holding the slot and the two ways forward: delete an event, or upgrade.

## Your events

The events you host and the events you added photos to, together, newest first. They draw as cover cards, or as rows for a longer list; the switch sits to the right of <UiLabel>Your events</UiLabel>, and this device remembers your choice. The rows add a sort menu: <UiLabel>Newest</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Most waiting</UiLabel>, or by name.

- **An event you host** shows its name, date, how many items are in the album (approved ones; anything waiting in Review isn't counted), and whether it's <UiLabel>Open</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>Closed</UiLabel> to new uploads. An amber chip in the corner tells you when uploads are waiting for review, and the small QR chip opens the share dialog without leaving the dashboard.
- **An event you added photos to** is marked <UiLabel>Guest</UiLabel> and shows who's hosting it. It stays for as long as you have an upload there, even one still waiting for the host's review, and leaves with your last one. If the host makes it private, the card locks; a password-protected event shows no cover and opens at its password. See [find your uploads, and the events you added to](/help/find-your-uploads-and-events).

The <UiLabel>Show</UiLabel> menu narrows the list: <UiLabel>All events</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Guest</UiLabel> for only the events you added to, or <UiLabel>Deleted</UiLabel>, the events you've deleted, each with a countdown until it clears for good. See [hide, remove, and restore](/help/hide-remove-and-restore).

If you typed your email at an event before you confirmed it, a card, <UiLabel>Photos waiting for you</UiLabel>, sits above your events until you decide what was yours.

## Just arrived

At the foot, the newest approved photos across the events you host, in a window that widens until it holds twelve: the last hour, then today, then the newest overall, and the caption says which. With nothing approved yet, it isn't there.

## The bell and the account menu

The bell collects the few things that need a decision; see [notifications and the emails Partyreel sends](/help/notifications-and-emails). Your avatar opens the account menu: <UiLabel>Your profile</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Plan and storage</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Account</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Back to site</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Help center</UiLabel>, the theme switch, and <UiLabel>Sign out</UiLabel>.

<Callout type="tip" title="Nothing here yet?">

A brand-new account sees <UiLabel>Your first album starts here</UiLabel> and a single button, <UiLabel>Create your first event</UiLabel>. The rest fills in as you go.

</Callout>
