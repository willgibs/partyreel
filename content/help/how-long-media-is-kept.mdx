We designed Partyreel so you don't have to race a countdown. Your memories aren't on a timer.

## Events stay until you delete them

There's no expiry date on an event. It stays up — album, photos, videos, and reel — until you choose to delete it. Deleting is also the only way to free up a storage slot or reclaim space, because it removes the underlying files for good.

<Callout type="warning" title="Deleting is permanent">

Removing an event or a media item deletes the original files. Save anything you want to keep first — see [downloading your photos and videos](/help/download-photos-videos-and-albums).

</Callout>

## A note on free events

To keep things tidy, free events that go completely untouched for a long stretch may eventually be archived — and if that's ever about to happen, we'll email you first so you have time to download or upgrade. Active events and paid plans aren't affected.

## Event Pass

An Event Pass keeps its event for about a year from purchase, which is plenty for a wedding album you'll revisit. If you need it longer, you can move to [Pro](/help/pro-vs-event-pass) before it lapses.

Worried about a specific photo rather than timing? See [reporting content and staying safe](/help/reporting-and-safety).
