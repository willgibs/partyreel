We designed Partyreel so you don't have to race a countdown. Your memories aren't on a timer.

## Events stay until you delete them

There's no expiry date on an event. It stays up, with its album, photos, videos, and reel, until you choose to delete it. Deleting is also how you free up a storage slot or reclaim space: the room comes back the moment you delete, and the files themselves are removed for good once the recovery window below runs out.

## Deleting comes with a 30-day safety net

Deleting isn't instantly final. A deleted event, or any removed photo or video, moves to the **Trash** for 30 days. During that window you can restore it and it comes back exactly as it was. After 30 days it's permanently deleted, and you can also permanently delete items from the Trash sooner if you want them gone now.

<Callout type="warning" title="After 30 days, it's gone for good">

Once the recovery window passes (or you clear the Trash yourself), the original files are permanently deleted and can't be recovered. Save anything you want to keep first; see [downloading your photos and videos](/help/download-photos-videos-and-albums).

</Callout>

## A note on free events

To keep things tidy, a free event that goes completely untouched for about six months gets an email warning a couple of weeks ahead, and then moves to the Trash, where the same 30-day restore window applies. Active events and paid plans aren't affected.

## Event Pass

An Event Pass keeps its event for about a year from purchase, which is plenty for a wedding album you'll revisit. If you need it longer, you can move to [Pro](/help/pro-vs-event-pass) before it lapses.

Worried about a specific photo rather than timing? See [reporting content and staying safe](/help/reporting-and-safety).
