Open an event from your dashboard and you're looking at the same album your guests see, with the host controls layered on. Nothing is hidden in tabs: the page is one scroll, filtered by pills.

<Path>Dashboard › Your event</Path>

## The header

The event name, its date, and a row of stats: how many photos and videos are in the album, how many people have contributed, and views. Views counts every open of the event link, including scans of the QR code and your own visits. Two chips describe the event's current state: its visibility (<UiLabel>Open</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Password</UiLabel>, or <UiLabel>Private</UiLabel>) and whether it's <UiLabel>Accepting uploads</UiLabel> or has <UiLabel>Uploads paused</UiLabel>. Hover either for the sentence your guests would experience.

## The command strip

- <UiLabel>Share</UiLabel> opens the QR code and the link, with <UiLabel>Customize</UiLabel> for the QR style. See [customize and share your QR code](/help/customize-and-share-your-qr).
- <UiLabel>Add photos</UiLabel> opens your own upload panel. See [add your own photos](/help/add-your-own-photos).
- <UiLabel>Settings</UiLabel> goes to the settings page. See [event settings, explained](/help/event-settings-explained).

## The sections

The pills <UiLabel>All</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Review</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Gallery</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Reel</UiLabel>, and <UiLabel>Guests</UiLabel> filter the page to one section or stack them all.

- **Review** appears at the top of the stack only while you review uploads and something is waiting; otherwise it sinks to the bottom as a quiet "all caught up" line, or a one-tap offer to <UiLabel>Turn on review</UiLabel>. See [review uploads before they appear](/help/review-uploads-before-they-appear).
- **Gallery** is the album itself, in the same masonry your guests see, with hover controls on each tile and a <UiLabel>Select</UiLabel> button for batch actions. See [curate what shows up in your album](/help/moderate-and-curate-your-album).
- **Reel** is where the highlight reel is born and, once it exists, where its poster and sharing card live. See [the highlight reel](/help/the-highlight-reel).
- **Guests** names the signed-in people who added photos, once you turn on the guest list. Until then it offers <UiLabel>Guest list settings</UiLabel>.

## The floating action bar

Scroll and a bar appears at the bottom that changes with the section under your thumb: <UiLabel>Add photos</UiLabel> over the gallery, <UiLabel>Approve all</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>Select</UiLabel> over Review, <UiLabel>Create reel</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>Open studio</UiLabel> over the reel. In Select mode it becomes the batch toolbar. See [select many photos at once](/help/bulk-select-and-batch-actions).

<Callout type="info" title="Your view versus theirs">

Guests see the gallery, the reel once you've shared it, and the guest list if it's on. They never see Review, hidden items, the stats row, or the like counts. To see the page as a guest, open the event link in a private browser window.

</Callout>
