Open an event from your dashboard and you're looking at the same album your guests see, with your controls around it: a live code at the top, a row of cards into the rest of the event, and the album itself filling the page beneath them. Nothing is hidden behind tabs or filters.

<Path>Dashboard › Your event</Path>

## The header

A real, scannable code sits at the left of the event's name, ready for a guest's camera exactly as it would be on a table card; tap it yourself for a bigger version, a copy-link button, your phone's own share sheet, and an <UiLabel>Everything</UiLabel> button into the full share panel with the QR designer and a readable custom link. Pausing uploads dims the code and marks it <UiLabel>Paused</UiLabel> right on the code, rather than in a separate chip. Beside it: the date, how many photos and videos are in the album, how many people have contributed, and views (every open of the event link, including QR scans and your own visits). A quieter link underneath shows the readable address and copies the permanent one in a tap, and the app bar above carries a trail back to your dashboard.

## The cards

Four cards open the rest of the event, each with a live line under its name: <UiLabel>Review</UiLabel> ("All caught up", or a waiting count), <UiLabel>Highlight reel</UiLabel> (<UiLabel>Live for guests</UiLabel> once it plays, or how many photos it still needs), <UiLabel>Guests</UiLabel> (a contributor count, or an offer to turn the list on), and <UiLabel>Settings</UiLabel> (the event's visibility). Review and Guests each open their own page; the Highlight reel opens the reel itself, exactly as your guests watch it (before its second photo, it shows what's left instead); Settings opens right over the album, so whatever you're changing stays in view behind it. See [review uploads before they appear](/help/review-uploads-before-they-appear), [the highlight reel](/help/the-highlight-reel), [profiles, guest lists, and following](/help/profiles-guest-lists-and-following), and [event settings, explained](/help/event-settings-explained). Scroll and the row rides along with you, condensing into a slim bar so it's never far.

## The album

Everything approved lives right below the cards, in one grid, newest first. Its own header carries <UiLabel>Add photos</UiLabel> for your own uploads, <UiLabel>Download</UiLabel> once there's something to download, a control for tile size, <UiLabel>Select</UiLabel> for batch actions, and a <UiLabel>Deleted</UiLabel> toggle that fetches anything removed on demand, restorable for <RecoveryDays />. See [add your own photos](/help/add-your-own-photos), [curate what shows up in your album](/help/moderate-and-curate-your-album), and [select many photos at once](/help/bulk-select-and-batch-actions).

<Callout type="info" title="Your view versus theirs">

Guests see the album, its highlight reel, and the guest list if it's on. They never see the Review queue, hidden items, the header's stats, or like counts. To see the page as a guest, open the event link in a private browser window.

</Callout>
