You entered your email, the screen says <UiLabel>Enter your code</UiLabel>, and the inbox is empty. Nearly every time it's one of five things, in this order.

## Check these first

<Steps>
<Step title="Look in spam, junk, and promotions">
A first email from a new sender is the usual thing a spam filter catches. Search the whole mailbox for "Partyreel".
</Step>
<Step title="Re-read the address on the screen">
The code screen shows exactly where it went. A one-letter typo is the most common cause; <UiLabel>Use a different email</UiLabel> takes you back to fix it.
</Step>
<Step title="Give it a minute, then tap Resend code">
Delivery lags on busy networks and with some providers. <UiLabel>Resend code</UiLabel> is available again after a short countdown; use the newest email once it arrives, since older codes stop working when a new one is sent.
</Step>
<Step title="Try the link instead of the code">
The same email carries a sign-in link. If the code seems wrong, the link is a second door.
</Step>
<Step title="Work or school inbox? Use a personal one">
Corporate mail systems quarantine automated messages more than most, especially at work events. A personal address gets through.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Tapped Resend too many times?

Resending too fast shows <UiLabel>Too many tries for now.</UiLabel> in place of the resend row, with a live countdown and a <UiLabel>Contact us</UiLabel> button underneath if you'd rather not wait. It clears itself the moment the countdown ends, and a code you already received keeps working until a newer one is sent.

## If it still won't come

Nothing is lost: whatever you picked to upload is still on your phone, ready to add once you're in. If codes repeatedly fail to arrive at an address you know is right, [tell us](/contact) which event you were joining (the other ways into an account are in [sign in: a code, Google, or a password](/help/sign-in-options-and-passwords)) and roughly when you tried, and we'll look into it. Every note gets a reply, usually within a day.

## A note for hosts

Guests see this step only when your event requires verified emails, which is the default. It's what confirms the name and puts an email behind every upload; the cost is this one extra step for guests. If a guest can't get a code at all and you'd rather have their photos than a confirmed email, you can turn the requirement off in settings; guests then just type a display name instead. See [require verified emails, explained](/help/require-verified-emails-explained).
