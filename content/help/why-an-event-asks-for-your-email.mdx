You scanned the code, you can see a handful of photos, and the page is asking for your email. That's the host's choice, not a paywall: they've asked that everyone who sees the full album and adds to it confirms who they are, once, with an email.

## What you'll see

The album sits behind the welcome screen, showing its newest <TeaserCount /> photos as a preview. <UiLabel>Continue</UiLabel> asks what to call you first, and then, on this kind of event, for your email, with a short explanation: the host keeps this album for guests only, so you confirm an email once, with no password.

The name comes before the email on purpose: it's the one that goes on your photos, and typing it takes a second. If you already have a Partyreel account, its name is the one that shows.

<Steps>
<Step title="Enter your email and tap Email me a code">
The page shows exactly which address the code went to.
</Step>
<Step title="Type the 6-digit code from the email">
Or tap the link in the same email; either one works. If it doesn't arrive within a minute, see [the email code didn't arrive](/help/the-email-code-didnt-arrive).
</Step>
<Step title="You're in">
The last step asks for a first photo, and then the album opens with <UiLabel>Add photos</UiLabel> on every screen. Only the host of this event ever sees your email; other guests see your name.
</Step>
</Steps>

## That code made you an account

There's no separate sign-up. Confirming your email creates a free Partyreel account, which is what lets you come back to this album later, save it to a dashboard, and keep your uploads under your name across events. Next time, the same email (or <UiLabel>Continue with Google</UiLabel> with the same address) opens the same account; the ways in are in [sign in: a code, Google, or a password](/help/sign-in-options-and-passwords). See [save an event, and find your uploads later](/help/save-an-event-and-find-your-uploads).

## Guests never pay

Nothing a guest does costs anything, ever: joining, adding photos, downloading, watching the reel. The host pays for storage if they need more of it. The <UiLabel>Start for free</UiLabel> button in the corner is for people who want to host their own event.

## Rather not?

There's no way past it on this event: the preview is all there is without the email, and the welcome screen stays in front of it. If the host had turned that requirement off, the page would have asked only for a name, with an email as an optional add, never required and nothing sent to it; typing one just means these photos are waiting for you if you ever confirm that address from an account. It's their setting. What they see about you is in [what guests can and can't see](/help/what-guests-can-and-cant-see).

<Callout type="info" title="A password instead?">

Some events ask for a password from the invitation rather than, or before, an email. That's a different gate: see [messages guests might see](/help/messages-guests-might-see).

</Callout>
