Creating an event is a one-minute job. Partyreel asks for a name, lets you pick a look for the QR code, and hands you the link. Every other setting starts with a sensible default you can change later.

## Before the wizard

The first time you sign in, Partyreel asks for the name your guests will see (<UiLabel>Add your name</UiLabel>) and offers a three-screen tour you can skip. Then you land on the dashboard. Tap <UiLabel>New event</UiLabel>.

<Path>Dashboard › New event</Path>

## Step 1: Details

- <UiLabel>Event name</UiLabel> is the only required field. Up to 80 characters; it's the title guests see when they scan.
- <UiLabel>Description (optional)</UiLabel> is a note guests see when they join. Good for "add everything from the ceremony too" or "photos only, please".
- <UiLabel>Event date (optional)</UiLabel> is for your reference. It doesn't open or close anything: uploads work before, during, and after the date.

Tap <UiLabel>Continue</UiLabel>.

## Step 2: Design

Pick one of four QR styles: <UiLabel>Classic</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Bold</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Rounded</UiLabel>, or <UiLabel>Dots</UiLabel>. Every one scans reliably (dark modules on white, always), so choose on looks. You can change it later from the share dialog. Tap <UiLabel>Create event</UiLabel>.

## Step 3: Share

Your event exists now, with its permanent link and a live, scannable QR code. Copy the link, or come back for it any time. Paid plans can also set a custom link here; see [set a custom event link](/help/custom-event-link). Tap <UiLabel>Go to your event</UiLabel>.

## The defaults you inherit

A new event starts out:

- **Public**: anyone with the link can view. The link is never listed or indexed, so "public" means your guests, not the internet.
- **Accepting uploads**, and showing them live, without review.
- **Requiring accounts to upload**: guests confirm an email before seeing the full album and adding photos. It's free, and it's what puts a name on every upload.
- **No cap on file size** beyond the universal <UploadSize /> per file.
- **Never password-protected** at creation; you can add a password afterward on a paid plan.

All of these live in event settings; see [event settings, explained](/help/event-settings-explained).

## One host per event

An event belongs to the account that created it. There's no co-host or shared-admin role yet, so if a partner, planner, or photographer needs to run the album, create it from the account you'll all sign into. A photographer can still add their batch as a guest, or you can add it yourself; see [add your own photos](/help/add-your-own-photos).

<Callout type="info" title="Events have no end date">

An event stays up until you delete it. On the free plan, an event nobody touches for <InactivityMonths /> is warned by email, then moved to Deleted, where it can be restored for <RecoveryDays />. Signing in is enough to keep it active.

</Callout>
