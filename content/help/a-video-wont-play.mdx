There's no "processing" state for video on Partyreel: a video is in the album the moment it uploads, as the original file. That's why it plays instantly on most phones, and why occasionally a particular file won't play in a particular browser.

## First, tap it

Videos never autoplay. In the gallery a video shows a still with a play badge; open it and tap the play control. If you see a black frame with controls, it's loaded and waiting for the tap.

## It's the format

Because nothing is converted, the video plays only if the browser can decode it. The common case: iPhones record by default in a format called HEVC (H.265), saved as a MOV file. Safari on any Apple device plays it; Chrome and Firefox on Windows, Android, and older Macs often can't, and show a stuck or black player. The same video works fine when downloaded and opened in a video app, so:

- **Viewers**: tap <UiLabel>Save</UiLabel> in the photo viewer and open the file in your phone's video app, or in a player like VLC on a computer.
- **Uploaders**: the iPhone's camera settings offer "Most Compatible", which records H.264 and plays everywhere. It's a good default for anything you'll share.

Hosts downloading the album get the original files, which open in any video app regardless of the browser. The accepted formats are in [what you can upload](/help/what-you-can-upload).

## It's the connection

A video streams as it plays, so a weak connection stutters rather than fails. Wait a moment, or download it.

## There's no video at all

Some events accept photos only, and a video you tried to add would have been refused with a message saying so. If a guest says they uploaded a video and it's missing, that's usually why; otherwise see [a photo is missing from the album](/help/a-photo-is-missing-from-the-album).

<Callout type="info" title="In the reel">

In the highlight reel a video plays as moving pictures with no sound, drawn by your own device. A device that can't play a particular video there shows its still frame instead, and the reel carries on. See [the highlight reel](/help/the-highlight-reel).

</Callout>
