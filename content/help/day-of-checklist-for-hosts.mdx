The album mostly runs itself. These are the handful of things worth doing before and during the event, in order. Ticks stay on this device, so you can come back to the list on the day.

<Checklist id="day-of-checklist-for-hosts">
<Check title="Decide whether to review uploads">
For a wedding or anything with a slideshow on a screen, turn on <UiLabel>Review uploads before they appear</UiLabel> so nothing goes public before you've seen it. For a casual party, leave it off and let the album fill live. See [review uploads before they appear](/help/review-uploads-before-they-appear).
</Check>
<Check title="Print the QR code, big">
Download the SVG from the share dialog and print it at least the size of a coaster; bigger for a sign across a room. Keep the white border around it. See [print your QR, or put it on a screen](/help/print-or-display-your-qr).
</Check>
<Check title="Test it as a guest">
Scan the printed code with your own phone in a private browser window (signed in, you'd see the host view and skip the gate). Add one photo and check it lands. Do this before anything is laminated.
</Check>
<Check title="Put it where people already look">
Table cards, the bar, the welcome sign, the back of the program, the bathroom mirror. Two or three places beat one big poster.
</Check>
<Check title="Tell everyone once, early">
A line in the program or a single announcement is enough. Something like: "Scan the code on your table to add your photos to our album. No app, no sign-up, just your camera." Copy the event link too, for the group chat. See [send the event link](/help/send-the-event-link).
</Check>
<Check title="Seed the album">
Add a few photos yourself before guests arrive. An empty album asks people to go first; one with five photos in it invites them. See [add your own photos](/help/add-your-own-photos).
</Check>
<Check title="Glance at Review during the event">
If you turned review on, open the event page on your phone every hour or so and tap <UiLabel>Approve all</UiLabel>. The bell on your dashboard counts what's waiting.
</Check>
<Check title="Close uploads when it's over">
Days later, once the last photos have trickled in, turn off <UiLabel>Accepting uploads</UiLabel>. The album becomes a keepsake page, and the reel moves to the top of it. See [share the album after the event](/help/share-the-album-after-the-event).
</Check>
</Checklist>

## If something goes wrong on the day

The troubleshooting guides cover the usual snags: [the QR won't scan](/help/the-qr-wont-scan-or-the-link-wont-open), [an upload won't finish](/help/an-upload-wont-finish), and [the messages guests might see](/help/messages-guests-might-see). And every note to us gets a reply, usually within a day.
