The reel is what the album becomes the morning after: the best of everything everyone shot, cut into a video short enough to send. It's built in your browser, it's free on every plan, and it starts with one tap.

## Make the first cut

<Path>Dashboard › Your event › Reel</Path>

Scroll to the Reel section of your event page. Partyreel offers a fill: <UiLabel>Start with the crowd favorites</UiLabel> once your guests have liked enough photos (see [likes: who sees them and what they do](/help/likes-and-what-they-do)), or <UiLabel>Start with a first cut</UiLabel> otherwise. Tap it and a balanced set is picked for you: what got liked, what's recent, a spread across the people who contributed, and a mix of photos and video stills. You can also hand-pick with <UiLabel>Select</UiLabel> in the gallery and <UiLabel>Add to reel</UiLabel>. Then tap <UiLabel>Create reel</UiLabel>.

The reel plays right there in its default style, and the section becomes its poster with an <UiLabel>Open studio</UiLabel> button.

## Shape it in the Studio

The Studio is the reel editor, with a panel for each decision: <UiLabel>Moments</UiLabel> (which photos, in what order), <UiLabel>Style</UiLabel> (14 to choose from), <UiLabel>Cover</UiLabel> (the opening shot), <UiLabel>Length</UiLabel>, and <UiLabel>Layout</UiLabel> (portrait or landscape). Every change re-cuts the reel instantly, and what you see is exactly what the downloaded video will be. See [pick the shots and put them in order](/help/pick-and-reorder-reel-moments) and [reel styles, length, cover, and layout](/help/reel-styles-length-and-layout).

## Download it, share it

<UiLabel>Download video</UiLabel> makes an MP4 on your own device in a few seconds. <UiLabel>Share with guests</UiLabel> puts the reel on the event page for everyone with access, where they can watch it and download their own copy. See [download the reel as a video](/help/download-the-reel-as-a-video) and [share the reel with your guests](/help/share-the-reel-with-guests).

## The limits

- **It's silent.** No music, no audio track. Add a song in whatever app you post from.
- **Videos show a still.** A video clip in the reel appears as its opening frame, not as moving footage.
- **One reel per event.** Change it as much as you like; there isn't a second one.
- **Free-plan reels run up to <ReelSeconds tier="free" /> seconds and carry a small partyreel.com mark.** Paid plans get <ReelSeconds tier="pro" /> seconds and no mark.

<Callout type="tip" title="It's the thing people re-watch">

A shared album is wonderful. A reel is the thing people send each other the next morning. Make one even for a small event; a first cut takes a minute.

</Callout>
