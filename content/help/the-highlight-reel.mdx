The highlight reel is what gives Partyreel its name. When the night winds down, all those scattered clips and photos become one short, polished video of the moments worth remembering — compiled automatically, and shaped by you.

## How it comes together

Partyreel looks across everything your guests captured and pulls together the standout moments into a single reel. You don't have to sit in an editor or sift through hundreds of files — the first cut is made for you.

<Callout type="tip" title="It's the payoff of the whole night">

A shared album is wonderful. A reel is the thing people actually re-watch and send to each other the next morning.

</Callout>

## You choose what makes the cut

The reel is yours to shape. Keep the automatic selection, or step in and choose exactly which moments are included and which are left out. It's your event — the final cut should feel like it.

## Share it or keep it

Once your reel is ready, it's as easy to pass around as anything else in Partyreel:

- **Share** it with a link, so guests can watch without digging through the album.
- **Download** it as a finished video to keep or post wherever you like.

The individual photos and videos always stay [downloadable on their own](/help/download-photos-videos-and-albums) too — the reel is a bonus on top, not a replacement. Want the bigger picture first? See [how Partyreel works](/help/how-partyreel-works).
