The highlight reel is what gives Partyreel its name. When the event winds down, everything your guests captured becomes one short, polished video of the moments worth remembering: built in seconds, shaped by you.

## How it comes together

Start the reel with one tap and Partyreel builds the first cut for you, pulling a balanced mix from across the album: crowd favorites, every contributor represented, a spread across the whole event. It plays instantly right in your browser, and what you preview is exactly what the finished video looks like, pixel for pixel.

<Callout type="tip" title="It's the payoff of the whole event">

A shared album is wonderful. A reel is the thing people actually re-watch and send to each other the next morning.

</Callout>

## Pick one of 14 styles

Every reel comes in your choice of 14 cinematic styles, and switching between them re-cuts the reel instantly:

- **Eight moods** keep your media front and center: Cinematic, Film, Pulse, Kinetic, Editorial, Sunset, Noir, and Float.
- **Six treatments** give it a designed look: Polaroid stack, Film strip, Scattered prints, Framed gallery, Card deck, and Layered parallax.

Portrait or landscape, every style adapts to the orientation you choose.

## You choose what makes the cut

The reel is yours to shape. Keep the automatic selection, or add and remove moments and drag them into exactly the order you want. It's your event, and the final cut should feel like it.

## Share it or keep it

- **Share it with your guests.** Turn on sharing and the reel appears right on the event page, where every guest can watch it and download it to keep.
- **Download it.** The finished video renders on your own device in seconds, free, from any modern browser. Free events make a 30-second reel with a small partyreel.com mark; Pro and Event Pass stretch it to a full minute with no mark.

The individual photos and videos always stay [downloadable on their own](/help/download-photos-videos-and-albums) too; the reel is a bonus on top, not a replacement. Want the bigger picture first? See [how Partyreel works](/help/how-partyreel-works).
