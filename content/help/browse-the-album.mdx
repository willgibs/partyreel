The event page is the album. It fills up while you watch, it's yours to browse as long as the host keeps it up, and nothing on it costs anything.

## The gallery

Photos and videos appear newest first, in a grid, within seconds of someone adding them. There's no refresh button because there's nothing to refresh: the page listens for new uploads. If the host is reviewing uploads first, only approved ones appear. A blurred preview under a sheet means you haven't finished at the door yet: the sheet says what's left, an email check or a first photo of your own (see [why an event asks for your email](/help/why-an-event-asks-for-your-email)).

Videos show a play badge and play when you open them, never on their own.

## The photo viewer

Tap anything to open it full-screen. Swipe (or tap the left and right edges) to move between photos; tap the middle, or the X, to close. At the bottom:

- **Like**: the heart. Only the host sees how many likes a photo has; it's a quiet way to tell them which shots were the good ones. See [likes: who sees them and what they do](/help/likes-and-what-they-do).
- <UiLabel>Save</UiLabel>: downloads the original, full-size file to your phone.
- <UiLabel>Share</UiLabel>: sends the event link, so a friend lands on the whole album. It never shares a single photo file.

Under the photo you'll see who added it, confirmed or wearing the small unverified mark, and a counter of where you are in the album.

## The reel

Once the album has two photos, its cover dissolves through the reel's own photos, and the cover's round play button, <UiLabel>Watch the highlight reel</UiLabel>, fills the screen with the reel: the whole album as a looping montage, taking in each new upload as it lands, yours included. Deep in the album, the same button waits beside the floating <UiLabel>Add photos</UiLabel>. Tap the picture to stop on a photo, or open the controls to pick your own style and pace (only your screen changes). <UiLabel>Make your own</UiLabel> turns the reel into a clip you keep. See [the highlight reel](/help/the-highlight-reel) and [make your own clip](/help/make-your-own-clip). No play button means the album has fewer than two photos, or the host switched the reel off.

## Take photos home

<UiLabel>Select</UiLabel>, just above the gallery beside <UiLabel>View</UiLabel>, turns every tile into a check. Tap the photos you want (<UiLabel>All</UiLabel> for everything, and once you've added some, <UiLabel>Yours</UiLabel> for just your own), then press Save at the foot. On a phone you choose <UiLabel>Save to Photos</UiLabel>, a light phone-size copy, or <UiLabel>Save to Files</UiLabel>, the originals in one zip; at a computer, Save gives you the zip. A very large pick comes in parts, and the download walks you through them. Details in [download your photos, videos, and albums](/help/download-photos-videos-and-albums).

## Invite someone

<UiLabel>Invite</UiLabel>, the round QR button on the cover, shows the same QR code and link the host has, so you can pass the album on to someone who wasn't there.

<Callout type="info" title="On a shared phone?">

If you signed in to see the album on someone else's phone, use <UiLabel>Sign out</UiLabel> in the account menu before you hand it back. The page goes back to the preview, and so does the next person's: signing out puts down the whole ticket, not just the sign-in.

</Callout>
