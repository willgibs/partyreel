The event page is the album. It fills up while you watch, it's yours to browse as long as the host keeps it up, and nothing on it costs anything.

## The gallery

Photos and videos appear newest first, in a two-column grid, within seconds of someone adding them. There's no refresh button because there's nothing to refresh: the page listens for new uploads. If the host is reviewing uploads first, only approved ones appear; if you're seeing only a preview, an email check unlocks the rest (see [why an event asks for your email](/help/why-an-event-asks-for-your-email)).

Videos show a play badge and play when you open them, never on their own.

## The photo viewer

Tap anything to open it full-screen. Swipe (or tap the left and right edges) to move between photos; tap the middle, or the X, to close. At the bottom:

- **Like**: the heart. Only the host sees how many likes a photo has; it's a quiet way to tell them which shots were the good ones. See [likes: who sees them and what they do](/help/likes-and-what-they-do).
- <UiLabel>Save</UiLabel>: downloads the original, full-size file to your phone.
- <UiLabel>Share</UiLabel>: sends the event link, so a friend lands on the whole album. It never shares a single photo file.

Under the photo you'll see who added it, or <UiLabel>Anonymous</UiLabel>, and a counter of where you are in the album.

## The reel

When the host has made a highlight reel and chosen to share it, it appears as a card on the page. Tap it to watch it full-screen; <UiLabel>Download</UiLabel> saves it as a video and <UiLabel>Share</UiLabel> sends the album link. Once the host closes uploads, the reel moves to the top of the page as the album's opening frame. If there's no card, the host hasn't shared one.

## Download everything

<UiLabel>Download all</UiLabel>, just above the gallery, bundles the album into a zip: everything, or just the photos or just the videos. Very large albums may need to be split by type. Details in [download your photos, videos, and albums](/help/download-photos-videos-and-albums).

## Invite someone

<UiLabel>Invite</UiLabel> under the event name shows the same QR code and link the host has, so you can pass the album on to someone who wasn't there.

<Callout type="info" title="On a shared phone?">

If you signed in to see the album on someone else's phone, use <UiLabel>Sign out</UiLabel> in the account menu before you hand it back. The page goes back to the preview.

</Callout>
