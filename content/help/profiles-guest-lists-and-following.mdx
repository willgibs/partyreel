Two things carry your name beyond your own photos: the event's guest list, which the host controls, and your profile page, which you control. Here's exactly what each shows.

## The guest list on an album

Every event page can have a <UiLabel>Guests</UiLabel> section naming the people who added photos, with their profile photos where they have one. It appears only if the host turns it on, and only to people who can already open the album. Everyone who added a photo is on it, handle or no handle, a confirmed name plain and a typed one wearing the small unverified mark. Emails are never listed; it's names and faces only.

At a big party the section shows a row of faces and how many guests added photos, and opening it lists the names a page at a time.

There's no per-guest opt-out on the event itself, and no way to add photos with no name attached at all; see [require verified emails, explained](/help/require-verified-emails-explained) for what the host's setting controls. If you'd rather not be on a list at all, the two ways are to not add photos to that event, or to have your uploads taken down: ask the host, or delete your own from your dashboard if you confirmed an account; see [save an event, and find your uploads later](/help/save-an-event-and-find-your-uploads).

## Your profile page

If you claim a profile handle, you get a page at `partyreel.com/u/your-name`. It shows your name and photo, one line about you if you write one, and one group of event cards: the events you host and choose to publish, marked <UiLabel>Host</UiLabel>, alongside open events you attended and chosen to show, where the host also turned on their guest list, marked <UiLabel>Guest</UiLabel>. The Guest cards show the event and its cover but do not open; being on a guest list is not a way into an album.

Nothing you attend shows on your profile until you turn it on there. You decide which joined events appear:

<Path>Account menu › Account › Public profile › Events you joined</Path>

Flip an event on and it appears on your profile; flip it back off (or never turn it on at all) and it stays private there. Either way that never takes you off the event's own guest list; the host controls that.

Without a handle, you have no public page at all: your name still appears on the albums you added photos to, with nothing behind it. Claiming a handle is free on every plan, and Partyreel offers you one right after your photos land; see [your public profile, following, and blocking](/help/your-public-profile-following-and-blocking).

## Following hosts

Anyone with an account can follow a host from their profile, and events the host publishes then show up under <UiLabel>Following</UiLabel> on your dashboard. Who follows whom is private to each person; there are no public follower counts anywhere on Partyreel.

## What stays private, always

Your email (only an event's host sees it, on their own dashboard), your likes, and your follows. The full list is in [what guests can and can't see](/help/what-guests-can-and-cant-see).
