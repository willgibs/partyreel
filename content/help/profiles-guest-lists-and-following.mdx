Every photo in a Partyreel album carries the name of the person who added it. That part is automatic. Everything past it, the guest list on the album, a public profile, following, is a choice, and this article walks through exactly who sees what.

## Your name in the album

Signed-in guests pick a display name once, and it travels with everything they add: on the tiles, in the lightbox, all through the album. The name is what the room sees; the email behind it stays with the host and is never shown in the album.

If a guest uploads without signing in (on events where the host allows it), their photos show as **Anonymous** instead. See [how guests join and upload](/help/how-guests-join-and-upload) for how signing in works.

## The guest list

Hosts see a live list of everyone who added photos while signed in: names and faces, not a spreadsheet, so you know who actually filled the album.

- By default, **the list is the host's alone**. Guests browsing the album don't see it.
- A host can flip **"Show the guest list on the album"** in the event's settings to share it with everyone who can open the album.
- Guests who uploaded anonymously are **never listed**, in either mode.

## Profiles are opt-in

A profile is a public page you choose to create, never something Partyreel makes for you.

- Claim a handle and you get a page at `partyreel.com/u/yourname` that carries your name from event to event.
- An event appears on your profile **only when you chose to show it**. Each event is its own decision.
- Skip profiles entirely and nothing about you is public: your name still appears inside albums you contributed to, and that's it.

<Callout type="info" title="The default is private">

No profile exists until you make one, and a new profile shows no events until you add them. Opting in is always the explicit step.

</Callout>

## Following

Profiles are how people stay connected after the event. Follow a host and the albums they publish show up in the Following section of your dashboard; follow the people you met and you'll recognize the names in the next gallery. Following someone shows you what they've chosen to make public, and nothing more.

## What stays private

- A guest's **email** stays with the host; the album only ever shows the display name.
- **Anonymous uploaders** are never named and never listed.
- The **guest list** stays host-only until the host deliberately shares it.
- **Profiles and their events** are opt-in, one event at a time.

For who can open the album itself, see [who can see your event](/help/who-can-see-your-event).
