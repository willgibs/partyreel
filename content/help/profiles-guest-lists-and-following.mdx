Two things carry your name beyond your own photos: the event's guest list, which the host controls, and your profile page, which you control. Here's exactly what each shows.

## The guest list on an album

Every event page can have a <UiLabel>Guests</UiLabel> section naming the people who added photos while signed in, with their profile photos. It appears only if the host turns it on, and only to people who can already open the album. Anonymous uploads are never listed, and neither are emails; it's names and faces only.

There's no per-guest opt-out on the event itself. If you'd rather not be on a list at all, the two ways are to add photos without signing in (when the host allows it) or to ask the host to remove your uploads.

## Your profile page

If you claim a profile handle, you get a page at `partyreel.com/u/your-name`. It shows your name and photo, the events you host and choose to publish, and, under "Also at", events you joined whose hosts turned on their guest list. Those "Also at" entries are names only, with no link into the album.

You choose which joined events appear there:

<Path>Account menu › Account › Public profile › Events you joined</Path>

Flip an event off and it disappears from your profile. That doesn't take you off the event's own guest list; the host controls that.

Without a handle, you have no public page at all. Claiming one is a paid feature; see [your public profile, following, and blocking](/help/your-public-profile-following-and-blocking).

## Following

Anyone with an account can follow a host from their profile, and events the host publishes then show up under <UiLabel>Following</UiLabel> on your dashboard. Who follows whom is private to each person; there are no public follower counts anywhere on Partyreel.

## What stays private, always

Your email (only an event's host sees it, on their own dashboard), your likes, your follows, and anything you uploaded anonymously. The full list is in [what guests can and can't see](/help/what-guests-can-and-cant-see).
