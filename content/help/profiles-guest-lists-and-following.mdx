Two things carry your name beyond your own photos: the event's guest list, which the host controls, and your profile page, which you control. Here's exactly what each shows.

## The guest list on an album

Every event page can have a <UiLabel>Guests</UiLabel> section naming the people who added photos while signed in, with their profile photos. It appears only if the host turns it on, and only to people who can already open the album. Everyone who added a photo while signed in is on it, handle or no handle. Anonymous uploads are never listed, and neither are emails; it's names and faces only.

At a big party the section shows a row of faces and how many guests added photos, and opening it lists the names a page at a time.

There's no per-guest opt-out on the event itself. If you'd rather not be on a list at all, the three ways are to add photos without signing in (when the host allows it), to ask the host to remove your uploads, or to delete your own uploads from your dashboard; see [save an event, and find your uploads later](/help/save-an-event-and-find-your-uploads).

## Your profile page

If you claim a profile handle, you get a page at `partyreel.com/u/your-name`. It shows your name and photo, one line about you if you write one, and one group of event cards: the events you host and choose to publish, marked <UiLabel>Host</UiLabel>, alongside open events you joined whose hosts turned on their guest list, marked <UiLabel>Guest</UiLabel>. The Guest cards show the event and its cover but do not open; being on a guest list is not a way into an album.

You choose which joined events appear there:

<Path>Account menu › Account › Public profile › Events you joined</Path>

Flip an event off and it disappears from your profile. That doesn't take you off the event's own guest list; the host controls that.

Without a handle, you have no public page at all: your name still appears on the albums you added photos to, with nothing behind it. Claiming a handle is free on every plan, and Partyreel offers you one right after your photos land; see [your public profile, following, and blocking](/help/your-public-profile-following-and-blocking).

## Following hosts

Anyone with an account can follow a host from their profile, and events the host publishes then show up under <UiLabel>Following</UiLabel> on your dashboard. Who follows whom is private to each person; there are no public follower counts anywhere on Partyreel.

## What stays private, always

Your email (only an event's host sees it, on their own dashboard), your likes, your follows, and anything you uploaded anonymously. The full list is in [what guests can and can't see](/help/what-guests-can-and-cant-see).
