A password puts a door on the link: the event's name and photo count show on the outside, and everything else waits until a guest types the password from your invitation.

<Path>Dashboard › Your event › Settings › Who can get in › A password</Path>

## Set a password

Under <UiLabel>What the link opens</UiLabel>, choose <UiLabel>Private</UiLabel>, then <UiLabel>A password</UiLabel> under <UiLabel>Who may join</UiLabel>. Type a password of at least <PasswordMinLength /> characters and tap <UiLabel>Set password</UiLabel>: setting it makes the password the album's gate in the same step, and the row then reads <UiLabel>Password is set</UiLabel>.

Partyreel never shows the password again, so write it down where you'll find it: it's the one thing to share with guests separately from the link, on the invitation or by message.

## What guests see

The locked page shows the event's name and how many photos are inside, over a ghosted grid. They tap <UiLabel>Unlock</UiLabel> after entering the password and the album opens. It stays open on that device for about <UnlockHours />, then asks again. A wrong password gets a gentle retry; five wrong tries in a row pause things for a few seconds. See [messages guests might see](/help/messages-guests-might-see).

If the album also asks for an email first, the password comes first and the email second.

## Guests already in

A guest who has joined the album (given a name or confirmed an email there) is in, and someone already in passes the password without typing it: on every phone they're signed in on, or on the phone they joined with. So a password added mid-party stops newcomers and leaves your guests where they are. Only <UiLabel>Only me</UiLabel>, or blocking someone, puts out a guest who's already in.

## Change or remove the password

<UiLabel>Change</UiLabel> sets a new password immediately: guests already in stay in, and anyone who had only unlocked the album needs the new one once their unlock expires. <UiLabel>Remove</UiLabel> takes the password off and makes the album Public. Both save on their own.

## Two things to know

- **Uploads respect the lock.** Nobody new can add a photo without having unlocked the album, even with a link that was working before you set the password.
- **It's on every plan,** the free plan included, so a password stays yours to set, change, or remove whatever you pay.

<Callout type="tip" title="A password, or another gate?">

A password is for guests you'd rather not ask for anything but a word: it's a door with a key. For a party where you'd rather see who's coming in, <UiLabel>You let each person in</UiLabel> holds every newcomer at the door until you say so, and <UiLabel>Your invite list</UiLabel> lets in the addresses you name. <UiLabel>Only me</UiLabel> is a wall: only you. See [who can see your event](/help/who-can-see-your-event).

</Callout>
