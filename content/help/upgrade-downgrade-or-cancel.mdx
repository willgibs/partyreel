Buying happens on the pricing page or from your plan in the app. Changing your Pro size, or switching between monthly and yearly, happens from <UiLabel>Change plan</UiLabel>. Your card, your invoices, and cancelling live in the billing portal, a secure page run by Stripe that Partyreel opens for you.

## Upgrade

Pick a plan on the [pricing page](/pricing), or press <UiLabel>Upgrade</UiLabel> on your account page, and check out. The moment the payment goes through, your account has the new storage and features: video, passwords, the longer reel. Nothing about your existing events changes; the links and QR codes you've printed keep working, and any paused uploads resume. Yearly Pro costs the price of ten months (two months free).

A plan has to hold what you already store. If your albums hold more than the size you picked, you're told how much you store and which size fits before anything is charged.

An Event Pass can be bought while you're on the free plan, or stacked on top of another pass; see [how long an Event Pass lasts](/help/how-long-an-event-pass-lasts). Pro holders can't add a pass, since Pro already covers it.

## Change size or switch to yearly

<Path>Account › Plan › Change plan</Path>

On Pro, <UiLabel>Change plan</UiLabel> on your account page (it's also in the storage meter on your dashboard) lists every Pro size, monthly and yearly, with <UiLabel>Your plan</UiLabel> marked. Press <UiLabel>Switch</UiLabel> beside the one you want: Stripe shows what changes on your next bill, and you confirm there. Picking a size on the pricing page brings you to the same confirmation; Pro is one subscription, resized rather than doubled.

A smaller size has to hold what you already store. If it can't, Partyreel shows how much you store and how much to remove first, and the switch opens once you're under. Removing frees space the moment you do it, so you never wait for Deleted to clear. A smaller size also shrinks Deleted, which keeps items only up to your plan's size.

## The billing portal

<Path>Dashboard › storage meter › Manage billing</Path>

Tap the storage meter on your dashboard, then <UiLabel>Manage billing</UiLabel>. (The button appears once you've paid for something.) The portal is where you:

- **Update your card**, and see your invoices and receipts. See [payments, receipts, and invoices](/help/payments-receipts-and-invoices).
- **Cancel.**

## Downgrade or cancel

Cancel Pro in the portal and your plan runs to the end of the period you've paid for, then the account settles back to the free plan. Your media stays put; nothing is deleted. If your albums hold more than <FreeStorage />, you get <OverCapGraceDays /> days to make room or come back before anything is trimmed. Password protection and a custom link you already set keep working, though you can't set new ones on the free plan. See [what happens when storage fills up](/help/what-happens-when-storage-fills-up).

Staying on Pro at a smaller size isn't a cancellation: it's a size change, above.

An Event Pass isn't cancelled; it simply runs its year.

<Callout type="info" title="Pass to Pro">

Moving from a pass to Pro converts the unused part of the pass into credit on your next Pro invoices. Buy Pro from the pricing page or your plan as usual; the credit is applied automatically. As with any plan, the Pro size you pick has to hold what your passes store now.

</Callout>
