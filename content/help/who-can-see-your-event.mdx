Your event is as private or as open as you want it to be. By default, the only way in is the link you share — and we work to keep that link out of search results.

## The link is the key

Your album opens to whoever has the share link. We deliberately keep those links out of search engines, so an album isn't something a stranger can stumble onto by searching. Share the link with your guests and it stays among them.

<Callout type="info" title="Public to your guests, not to the web">

"Public" in Partyreel means visible to people with your link — the guests at your event. It does not mean listed on the open internet.

</Callout>

## You control the switches

As the host, you decide:

- **Whether the album is visible** at all, and when to open it.
- **Whether you're still accepting uploads**, so you can close submissions when the event's over.
- **What's in it** — through [review and removal](/help/moderate-and-curate-your-album).

## Asking guests for an email

You can optionally ask guests for an email when they join. It's off by default — guests only need a display name unless you turn it on. For how long everything sticks around, see [how long your media is kept](/help/how-long-media-is-kept).
