Your event is as private or as open as you want it to be. By default, the only way in is the link you share, and we work to keep that link out of search results.

## The link is the key

Your album opens to whoever has the share link. We deliberately keep those links out of search engines, so an album isn't something a stranger can stumble onto by searching. Share the link with your guests and it stays among them.

<Callout type="info" title="Public to your guests, not to the web">

"Public" in Partyreel means visible to people with your link, the guests at your event. It does not mean listed on the open internet.

</Callout>

## You control the switches

As the host, you decide:

- **How open the event is.** Three levels: **Open** (anyone with the link), **Password-protected** (guests enter a password you set; a Pro and Event Pass feature), or **Private** (only you). A locked event shows a visitor nothing but the event name and a photo count.
- **Whether you're still accepting uploads**, so you can close submissions when the event's over.
- **What's in it**, through [review and removal](/help/moderate-and-curate-your-album).

## Requiring guests to verify an email

The **Require accounts** setting is on for new events: each guest confirms their email with a one-time code before seeing the full album or uploading, so every upload traces back to a verified guest. Until someone confirms, they see only a small preview of the newest photos. You can turn the setting off to allow fully anonymous viewing and uploads; anonymous uploads show as "Anonymous" in the album. Requiring accounts is free on every plan.

## Location data stays on the phone

Phone cameras stamp photos and videos with GPS coordinates and device details. Before an upload leaves a guest's phone, Partyreel strips that metadata right in the browser, without touching the image itself. So the full-quality originals you download and re-share don't carry where they were taken. For how long everything sticks around, see [how long your media is kept](/help/how-long-media-is-kept).
