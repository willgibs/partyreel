Every Partyreel album starts private in the way that matters: nobody can find it. From there, you decide how open it is to the people you give the link to.

## The link is the door

An event's address is a long random token. It isn't listed on Partyreel, it isn't linked from your profile unless you choose to publish it, and every event page asks search engines not to index it. So "anyone with the link" means your guests and whoever they pass it to, not the internet.

## Three levels

<Path>Dashboard › Your event › Settings › Visibility & access</Path>

- <UiLabel>Public</UiLabel>: anyone with the link can view the album. The default.
- <UiLabel>Password</UiLabel>: anyone with the link and the password you set. Guests see the event's name and how many photos are inside, nothing else, until they enter it. A <PlanBadge tier="paid" /> feature; see [password-protect your event](/help/password-protect-your-event).
- <UiLabel>Private</UiLabel>: only you. Guests see a friendly locked screen, and the link's preview card shows nothing. Useful while you curate.

## Whether guests confirm an email

Separately, <UiLabel>Require accounts to upload</UiLabel> (on by default, free on every plan) means a guest sees a preview of the newest <TeaserCount /> photos and confirms an email before seeing the rest or adding to it. Turn it off and anyone with the link can view and add anonymously. See [require accounts to upload, explained](/help/require-accounts-to-upload-explained).

The settings page shows one sentence describing exactly what a guest will experience with your current combination, and the event page's visibility chip shows the same sentence on hover.

## What's visible inside

Whoever can open the album sees approved photos with the uploader's display name (or <UiLabel>Anonymous</UiLabel>), never an email. Hidden and unapproved photos aren't sent to guests at all. Like counts are yours alone. The guest list, if you turn on <UiLabel>Show the guest list on the album</UiLabel>, names every signed-in contributor to anyone who can open the album; it's off until you say so. The full table is in [what guests can and can't see](/help/what-guests-can-and-cant-see).

## Location data

Photos carry hidden location and device details. Partyreel strips them in the browser before an upload leaves the phone, for the common formats, so the originals people download don't say where they were taken. See [photo metadata and location data](/help/photo-metadata-and-location).

<Callout type="tip" title="Leaked link?">

If a link gets somewhere it shouldn't, switch the event to <UiLabel>Private</UiLabel> or add a password. Either one locks the existing link in the same second; there's no way to make a new one, and no need.

</Callout>
