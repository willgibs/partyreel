Two ways to get a photo off the album, with one difference between them: hide keeps it where it is, remove starts the clock.

## Hide

<UiLabel>Hide</UiLabel> on a tile (or in the photo viewer on a phone) takes the photo off the album for guests instantly, with the toast <UiLabel>Hidden from everyone</UiLabel>. For you it stays in the gallery at 30 percent opacity with a persistent <UiLabel>Show</UiLabel> chip, so it's easy to find and one tap to put back. Hidden photos still count toward your storage and still appear in your downloads if you ask for them. A hidden photo also drops out of the highlight reel at once, and rejoins it the moment you Show it again.

Hide is for the blurry duplicate, the one shot someone would rather not see, and anything you're not sure about yet.

## Remove

<UiLabel>Remove</UiLabel> in the photo viewer, or <UiLabel>Delete</UiLabel> in Select mode, asks you to confirm: the photo disappears from the album right away and moves to Deleted, permanently gone after <RecoveryDays /> unless you restore it first. Its space comes back to your plan immediately.

## Deleted, and restoring

<Path>Dashboard › Your event › View › Deleted</Path>

Removed photos wait under the <UiLabel>Deleted</UiLabel> filter in the album's <UiLabel>View</UiLabel> menu for <RecoveryDays />, each with a countdown. <UiLabel>Restore</UiLabel> puts one back exactly as it was: a photo that was hidden comes back hidden, one that was waiting for review comes back waiting. <UiLabel>Delete permanently</UiLabel> skips the wait, after a confirmation that it can't be undone. Nothing in Deleted can be downloaded, so grab a copy before you remove something you might want.

Restoring needs room: if your storage is full, Restore refuses and offers an upgrade instead.

Deleted events work the same way: pick <UiLabel>Deleted</UiLabel> in the dashboard's <UiLabel>Show</UiLabel> menu, and a restore brings the whole album back in one go. See [your dashboard, explained](/help/your-dashboard-explained); deleting an event is in [event settings, explained](/help/event-settings-explained).

## What won't come back

- **Anything removed by Partyreel** after a report. It shows in Deleted, and Restore answers that the item is no longer available.
- **A guest's own deletion.** When a guest deletes their own upload, right on the album or from their profile, it's gone for you too: it leaves your album at once, it never appears in Deleted, and it isn't counted in the Deleted figure on your storage meter. There's nothing to restore. Their choice wins, and their confirmation told them so.

## Deleted has a budget

Deleted items are held free of charge, up to roughly your plan's storage size. Past that, the oldest items clear early rather than waiting the full <RecoveryDays />. The storage meter on your dashboard shows what's in Deleted at any time.

<Callout type="tip" title="Undo is the whole point">

Hide first when in doubt. Remove when you're sure. Delete permanently only when you need the space today.

</Callout>
