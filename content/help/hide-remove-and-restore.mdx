Two ways to get a photo off the album, with one difference between them: hide keeps it where it is, remove starts the clock.

## Hide

<UiLabel>Hide</UiLabel> on a tile (or in the photo viewer on a phone) takes the photo off the album for guests instantly, with the toast <UiLabel>Hidden from everyone</UiLabel>. For you it stays in the gallery at 30 percent opacity with a persistent <UiLabel>Show</UiLabel> chip, so it's easy to find and one tap to put back. Hidden photos still count toward your storage and still appear in your downloads if you ask for them. They can't be added to the reel, though a photo already in the reel stays there when hidden.

Hide is for the blurry duplicate, the one shot someone would rather not see, and anything you're not sure about yet.

## Remove

<UiLabel>Remove</UiLabel> in the photo viewer, or <UiLabel>Delete</UiLabel> in Select mode, asks you to confirm: the photo disappears from the album right away and is permanently deleted after a short grace period. Its space comes back to your plan immediately.

## Deleted, and restoring

<Path>Dashboard › Your event › Settings › Deleted</Path>

Removed photos sit in the event's <UiLabel>Deleted</UiLabel> card for <RecoveryDays />, each with a countdown. <UiLabel>Restore</UiLabel> puts one back exactly as it was: a photo that was hidden comes back hidden, one that was waiting for review comes back waiting. <UiLabel>Delete permanently</UiLabel> skips the wait, after a confirmation that it can't be undone. Nothing in Deleted can be downloaded, so grab a copy before you remove something you might want.

Restoring needs room: if your storage is full, Restore refuses and offers an upgrade instead.

Deleted events work the same way, on the dashboard's <UiLabel>Deleted</UiLabel> chip, with the whole album restored in one go. See [event settings, explained](/help/event-settings-explained).

## Two things you won't find in Deleted

- **A guest's own deletion.** When a signed-in guest deletes their own upload from their dashboard, it doesn't land in your bin and can't be restored. Their choice wins.
- **Anything removed by Partyreel** after a report. It shows as no longer available.

## The bin has a budget

Deleted items are held free of charge, up to roughly your plan's storage size. Past that, the oldest items clear early rather than waiting the full <RecoveryDays />. The storage meter on your dashboard shows what's in Deleted at any time.

<Callout type="tip" title="Undo is the whole point">

Hide first when in doubt. Remove when you're sure. Delete permanently only when you need the space today.

</Callout>
