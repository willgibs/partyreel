An event link is easy to lose. A saved event isn't: it lives on a dashboard tied to your email, with everything you've added and everything you've liked, across every event you've ever joined.

## Save an event

Tap <UiLabel>Save</UiLabel> under the event name. If you're not signed in, a small sheet offers to make a free account with the same email code (or <UiLabel>Continue with Google</UiLabel>), then saves the event and confirms with <UiLabel>Saved to your dashboard.</UiLabel> Tap the button again (now <UiLabel>Saved</UiLabel>) to remove it. Once the album opens, a card offering to keep what you just added makes the same offer; <UiLabel>Maybe later</UiLabel> dismisses it.

A private event can't be saved, and you can't save an event you host (it's already on your dashboard).

## Your dashboard

<Path>Account menu › Dashboard</Path>

- <UiLabel>Events</UiLabel> lists your saved events alongside any you host. If a host later makes an event private, its card locks.
- <UiLabel>Uploads</UiLabel> is every photo and video you've added to any event, newest first, up to your most recent 200. Tap one to open it, save it, or delete it.
- <UiLabel>Likes</UiLabel> is every photo you've hearted, on any event.

## Uploads you made before signing in

If you added photos under a typed display name and later confirm your email on the same phone and browser, Partyreel attaches those uploads to your account; the next time you open your dashboard it says <UiLabel>We added your uploads to your account.</UiLabel> They lose the small unverified mark and appear under Uploads. It works from the same phone and browser only, because that's what Partyreel can recognize as you; photos added from a different phone keep the typed name and stay unclaimed.

## Delete one of your own uploads

Open the photo, either right on the album or from <UiLabel>Uploads</UiLabel> on your dashboard, and tap delete. The confirmation reads <UiLabel>Delete this upload?</UiLabel> and explains it's removed from the event right away and permanently deleted after a short grace period. This works whether you're signed in or not: signed in, it's any upload of yours, in any event, from any device, ever; without signing in, it works only from the same phone and browser you uploaded from. Either way it's final, even for the host, who can't restore it once you've removed it. If neither applies (an unverified upload from a different phone), ask the host, who can still remove it.

<Callout type="tip" title="One account, both sides">

The same free account hosts events too. <UiLabel>New event</UiLabel> on your dashboard is the start of [how Partyreel works](/help/how-partyreel-works) from the other side.

</Callout>
