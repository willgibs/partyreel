An event link is easy to lose. A saved event isn't: it lives on a dashboard tied to your email, with everything you've added and everything you've liked, across every event you've ever joined.

## Save an event

Tap <UiLabel>Save</UiLabel> under the event name. If you're not signed in, a small sheet offers to make a free account with the same one-tap email code (or <UiLabel>Continue with Google</UiLabel>), then saves the event and confirms with <UiLabel>Saved to your dashboard.</UiLabel> Tap the button again (now <UiLabel>Saved</UiLabel>) to remove it. After you add photos, a card called <UiLabel>Keep these photos</UiLabel> offers the same thing; <UiLabel>Maybe later</UiLabel> dismisses it.

A private event can't be saved, and you can't save an event you host (it's already on your dashboard).

## Your dashboard

<Path>Account menu › Dashboard</Path>

- <UiLabel>Events</UiLabel> lists your saved events alongside any you host. If a host later makes an event private, its card locks.
- <UiLabel>Uploads</UiLabel> is every photo and video you've added to any event, newest first, up to your most recent 200. Tap one to open it, save it, or delete it.
- <UiLabel>Likes</UiLabel> is every photo you've hearted, on any event.

## Uploads you made before signing in

If you added photos anonymously and later confirm your email on the same phone and browser, Partyreel attaches those uploads to your account and tells you so with <UiLabel>We added your uploads to your account.</UiLabel> They gain your name in the album and appear under Uploads. It works from the same device only, because that's where the anonymous session lives; photos added from a different phone stay anonymous.

## Delete one of your own uploads

From <UiLabel>Uploads</UiLabel>, open the photo and choose delete. The confirmation reads <UiLabel>Delete this upload?</UiLabel> and explains it's removed from the event right away and permanently deleted after a short grace period. This is the only place a guest can delete their own upload; the event page itself has no delete. If you can't (the upload is anonymous, or it was another phone), ask the host, who can remove it instantly.

<Callout type="tip" title="One account, both sides">

The same free account hosts events too. <UiLabel>New event</UiLabel> on your dashboard is the start of [how Partyreel works](/help/how-partyreel-works) from the other side.

</Callout>
