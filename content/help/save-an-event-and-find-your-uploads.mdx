An event link is easy to lose. A saved event isn't: it lives on your dashboard, tied to your email, and everything you've added or liked across every event lives on your profile.

## Save an event

Tap <UiLabel>Save</UiLabel> under the event name. If you're not signed in, a small sheet offers to make a free account with the same email code (or <UiLabel>Continue with Google</UiLabel>), then saves the event and confirms with <UiLabel>Saved to your dashboard.</UiLabel> Tap the button again (now <UiLabel>Saved</UiLabel>) to remove it. Once the album opens, a card offering to keep what you just added makes the same offer; <UiLabel>Maybe later</UiLabel> dismisses it.

A private event can't be saved, and you can't save an event you host (it's already on your dashboard).

## Your dashboard

Tap the Partyreel logo, top left, from anywhere in the app. <UiLabel>Events</UiLabel> lists your saved events alongside any you host. If a host later makes an event private, its card locks.

## Your profile

<Path>Account menu › Your profile</Path>

Your own photos, likes, and the people you follow live on your profile page, private to you even though the rest of the page is public.

- <UiLabel>Your uploads</UiLabel> is every photo and video you've added to any event, newest first, up to your most recent 200. Tap one to open it, save it, or delete it.
- <UiLabel>Your likes</UiLabel> is every photo you've hearted, on any event.

## Uploads you made before signing in

If you added photos under a typed display name and later confirm your email on the same phone and browser, Partyreel attaches those uploads to your account; the next time you open your dashboard it says <UiLabel>We added your uploads to your account.</UiLabel> They lose the small unverified mark and appear under <UiLabel>Your uploads</UiLabel> on your profile. This works from the same phone and browser only, because that's what Partyreel can recognize as you.

## Claim photos added under your email, from any device

If you typed your email at an event that didn't need a confirmed one, it's held quietly against those uploads until you prove it's yours, however long that takes. Confirm that same address later, on any device, and your dashboard shows a card, <UiLabel>Photos waiting for you</UiLabel>, one row per event. Tap <UiLabel>Claim</UiLabel> on the ones that were really you, or <UiLabel>Not mine</UiLabel> on any that weren't, or tap <UiLabel>Claim all</UiLabel> to take every one in a single tap.

Tap <UiLabel>Finish</UiLabel> to apply your choices. Anything you claimed loses the unverified mark and moves under <UiLabel>Your uploads</UiLabel> on your profile. If you leave anything unclaimed, a confirmation asks before those photos and videos are permanently deleted, since leaving one unclaimed is you saying it wasn't yours; if someone else used your address without you, this is how you undo it.

## Delete one of your own uploads

Open the photo, either right on the album or from <UiLabel>Your uploads</UiLabel> on your profile, and tap delete. The confirmation reads <UiLabel>Delete this upload?</UiLabel> and explains it's removed from the event right away and permanently deleted after <RecoveryDays />. This works whether you're signed in or not: signed in, it's any upload of yours, in any event, from any device, ever; without signing in, it works only from the same phone and browser you uploaded from. Either way it's final, even for the host, who can't restore it once you've removed it. If neither applies (an unverified upload from a different phone), ask the host, who can still remove it.

<Callout type="tip" title="One account, both sides">

The same free account hosts events too. <UiLabel>New event</UiLabel> on your dashboard is the start of [how Partyreel works](/help/how-partyreel-works) from the other side.

</Callout>
