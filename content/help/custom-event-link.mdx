Every event has a permanent link made of a long random token. A custom link is a second address you choose, readable enough to put on a sign or say out loud. Both open the same event.

<Path>Dashboard › Your event › Settings › Event link</Path>

## Set one

In the <UiLabel>Event link</UiLabel> card, type your address after the `/e/` prefix in the <UiLabel>Custom link</UiLabel> field, or tap the suggestion Partyreel derives from the event name. It checks availability as you type and tells you whether the address is free or taken. Tap <UiLabel>Save link</UiLabel>.

The rules: lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens; between 3 and 50 characters; no hyphen at the start or end. A few words are reserved, and anything that looks like a permanent token is refused.

Custom links are a <PlanBadge tier="paid" /> feature. On the free plan the card shows an upgrade link instead of the field.

## What stays the same

The permanent link and the QR code never change. Printed codes stay valid whether you add, change, or remove a custom link, because the code always encodes the permanent address. The custom link is a convenience on top, not a replacement.

## Change or remove it

<UiLabel>Change</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>Remove</UiLabel> both ask you to confirm, and both come with the same warning: the current custom address stops working right away, with no redirect. Anyone who saved it needs the new one. That's also why a freed address becomes available to any other event.

If you later move to the free plan, an existing custom link keeps working and you can still remove it; you just can't change it or set a new one.

<Callout type="tip" title="Where a custom link earns its keep">

Under a QR code on a screen, in a spoken announcement, and on printed invitations that go out before the event exists. Everywhere else, the copied permanent link is just as good.

</Callout>
