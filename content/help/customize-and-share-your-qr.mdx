Your QR code is how guests get in. Partyreel lets you style it so it feels like part of the event, while keeping it rock-solid to scan.

## Pick a style

In the event designer, choose from a set of QR styles. Each one keeps the dark data on a clean light background — the part that actually matters for scanning — and uses the brand accent only to tint the corner markers. So your code can look distinct without ever becoming hard to read.

<Callout type="warning" title="Always test a scan">

If you customize a code, scan it once with your own phone before you print a hundred of them. A QR that looks great but won't scan is worse than a plain one.

</Callout>

## Ways to share it

- **On a screen** — put it up on a TV, projector, or laptop and guests scan from their seats.
- **Printed** — download the code and drop it onto table cards, a welcome sign, or a program.
- **As a link** — copy the join link and text or email it. Tapping it works just like scanning.

## What guests see

When someone scans or taps, they go straight to your event's join page — no app store, no login. They pick a display name and start uploading. Next up: [how guests join and upload](/help/how-guests-join-and-upload).
