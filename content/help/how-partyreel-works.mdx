Every event has a roomful of phones taking better photos than any one person could. Partyreel gathers them. You make an event, guests scan a code, and the album fills itself in while the night is still going.

## The loop

<Steps>
<Step title="Create the event, get a QR code">
Sign in, name your event, pick a QR style, and you're done. It takes about a minute, and the free plan covers <MaxEvents tier="free" /> with <FreeStorage /> of space. The event gets one permanent link, and the QR code is that link in scannable form. See [create your first event](/help/create-your-first-event).
</Step>
<Step title="Share one code">
Put it where people already are: a table card, a welcome sign, the screen at the front. One code covers the whole room, all evening; a guest just points their phone camera at it and the event opens in their browser, nothing to install. See [how guests join and upload](/help/how-guests-join-and-upload).
</Step>
<Step title="Watch it fill">
Photos and videos land in the album within seconds, for you and for every guest looking at it. Watch it fill from your own phone, or put it on a screen in the room.
</Step>
<Step title="Shape what shows">
Hide anything that shouldn't be there, remove anything that really shouldn't, or turn on Review so nothing shows until you've approved it. Everything is reversible for <RecoveryDays />. See [curate what shows up in your album](/help/moderate-and-curate-your-album).
</Step>
<Step title="Take it all home">
The same link that collected the photos is the album you share afterward. Anyone who can open it can download originals, one at a time or the whole set as a zip, photos and video together. See [download photos, videos, and albums](/help/download-photos-videos-and-albums).
</Step>
<Step title="Watch the reel grow">
From the second photo, the album plays as a highlight reel at the top of the page and on any screen in the room, taking in every upload as it lands. There's nothing to make: it's free on every plan, and anyone can turn it into a clip of their own with <UiLabel>Make your own</UiLabel>. See [the highlight reel](/help/the-highlight-reel).
</Step>
</Steps>

## What it costs

The free plan is a real plan: <MaxEvents tier="free" />, <FreeStorage /> of storage, photos only, and <ReelSeconds tier="free" />-second clips with a small Partyreel mark (the reel itself carries none). When you need more, Pro is a subscription with unlimited events and video, and the Event Pass is a one-time payment for a single big event. The full picture is in [what the free plan includes](/help/what-the-free-plan-includes) and [Pro vs. Event Pass](/help/pro-vs-event-pass).

## Where things live

- **Your dashboard** lists the events you host and the events you added photos to as a guest, with Deleted, where removed events wait, one menu away; your own uploads and likes live on your profile. See [your dashboard, explained](/help/your-dashboard-explained).
- **Each event page** is a hub: a live code beside the title, doors into Review, the Highlight reel, and Guests, and the album itself, filling the page. See [your event page, explained](/help/your-event-page-explained).
- **The event link** is the only door. It isn't listed anywhere and search engines are asked to stay out, so the album stays among the people you gave it to. See [who can see your event](/help/who-can-see-your-event).

<Callout type="tip" title="Start small">

Make a test event tonight, scan the code with your own phone, open the link in a private browser window so you see the guest side, and add three photos. Ten minutes with the real thing beats any tour, and the free plan is built for exactly this.

</Callout>
