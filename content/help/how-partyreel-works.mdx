Every event overflows with photos and videos, and almost all of them stay trapped on the cameras that took them. Partyreel fixes that. You share one QR code, your guests scan it, and everything they capture lands in a single shared album you control.

## The short version

1. **Create an event** and give it a name. That's the only required step.
2. **Share its QR code** on a screen, a printed card, or as a link.
3. **Guests scan and upload** straight from their phone browser. There's no app to install and nothing to chase down afterward.
4. **You curate** the album, download everything in one go, and turn the best moments into a [highlight reel](/help/the-highlight-reel).

<Callout type="tip" title="No friction for guests">

Guests never install anything. They scan and start uploading. If you keep the "Require accounts" setting on (it's on for new events), each guest confirms their email with a one-time code first, so you always know who added what.

</Callout>

## Why a shared album beats a group chat

Group chats compress your photos, scatter them across threads, and lose them the moment someone leaves. Partyreel keeps every upload in full quality, in one place, for as long as you want it.
