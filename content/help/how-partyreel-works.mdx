Every event has a roomful of phones taking better photos than any one person could. Partyreel gathers them. You make an event, guests scan a code, and the album fills itself in while the night is still going.

## The loop

<Steps>
<Step title="Create an event, get a QR code">
Sign in, name your event, pick a QR style, and you're done. It takes about a minute, and the free plan covers <MaxEvents tier="free" /> with <FreeStorage /> of space. The event gets one permanent link, and the QR code is that link in scannable form. See [create your first event](/help/create-your-first-event).
</Step>
<Step title="Guests scan and upload">
A guest points their phone camera at the code, the event opens in their browser, and they tap <UiLabel>Add photos</UiLabel>. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for beyond a quick email check if you ask for one. Photos upload at full quality, straight from the camera roll. See [how guests join and upload](/help/how-guests-join-and-upload).
</Step>
<Step title="Everything lands in one live album">
Uploads appear in the gallery within seconds, for you and for every guest looking at it. You can watch it fill up on your own phone, or put it on a screen in the room.
</Step>
<Step title="You curate">
Hide anything that shouldn't be there, remove anything that really shouldn't, or turn on Review so nothing shows until you've approved it. Everything is reversible for <RecoveryDays />. See [curate what shows up in your album](/help/moderate-and-curate-your-album).
</Step>
<Step title="Share it, download it, cut the reel">
The same link that collected the photos is the album you share afterward. Anyone who can open the album can download originals, one at a time or the whole set as a zip. And Partyreel turns the best moments into a short highlight reel, free on every plan. See [the highlight reel](/help/the-highlight-reel).
</Step>
</Steps>

## What it costs

The free plan is a real plan: <MaxEvents tier="free" />, <FreeStorage /> of storage, photos only, a <ReelSeconds tier="free" />-second reel with a small Partyreel mark. When you need more, Pro is a subscription with unlimited events and video, and the Event Pass is a one-time payment for a single big event. The full picture is in [what the free plan includes](/help/what-the-free-plan-includes) and [Pro vs. Event Pass](/help/pro-vs-event-pass).

## Where things live

- **Your dashboard** lists your events (and any you've saved as a guest), events from hosts you follow, your uploads, your likes, and Deleted, where removed items wait. See [your dashboard, explained](/help/your-dashboard-explained).
- **Each event page** is the album with your controls around it: share, add photos, review, and the reel. See [your event page, explained](/help/your-event-page-explained).
- **The event link** is the only door. It isn't listed anywhere and search engines are asked to stay out, so the album stays among the people you gave it to. See [who can see your event](/help/who-can-see-your-event).

<Callout type="tip" title="Start small">

Make a test event tonight, scan the code with your own phone, open the link in a private browser window so you see the guest side, and add three photos. Ten minutes with the real thing beats any tour, and the free plan is built for exactly this.

</Callout>
