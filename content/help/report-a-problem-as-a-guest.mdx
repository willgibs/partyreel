Most problems on an album are one photo that shouldn't be there, and the fastest fix is the person who owns the album. Reporting to Partyreel is the door for harm the host can't or won't handle.

## Ask the host first

The host has the final say over their album and can remove anything in one tap; it disappears for everyone immediately. (If you're the host, your controls are in [reporting content and keeping your event safe](/help/reporting-and-safety).) If it's your own photo you'd like gone, you can also delete it yourself, right on the album, and it's gone for good, for the host too; see [find your uploads, and the events you added to](/help/find-your-uploads-and-events).

## Report to Partyreel

Open the photo or video and tap <UiLabel>Report</UiLabel>, the flag beside Save and Share: the form opens with it named, so we know exactly what to look at. To report the album as a whole, use the small <UiLabel>Report</UiLabel> link at the very bottom of the event page, once you're past any password.

<Steps>
<Step title="Tap Report" screen="report-open">
A short form opens, titled for what you're reporting: <UiLabel>Report this photo</UiLabel>, or <UiLabel>Report this event</UiLabel> from the bottom of the page.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick what it is, and say what's wrong" screen="report-reason">
Choose the one that fits under <UiLabel>What is it?</UiLabel>; <UiLabel>Something else</UiLabel> covers the rest. The <UiLabel>Reason (optional)</UiLabel> box is for anything that helps: "the third photo from the top is of my child, and nobody asked us before posting it" is exactly the right kind of note.
</Step>
<Step title="Tap Submit report" screen="report-sent">
You'll see <UiLabel>Thanks. Your report has been sent for review.</UiLabel>
</Step>
</Steps>

You don't need an account, and the host is never told who reported. If you'd like us to be able to ask you for more, tap <UiLabel>Confirm your email</UiLabel> on the form and enter the code we send: it makes a free account, we write only if we need more from you, and your address is deleted from the report when it closes.

## What happens next

Every report gets a careful look, and we act where the harm is clear. A report that doesn't show it, or one about a photo that's simply unflattering, is closed without removing anything, so say what's wrong and why. Serious problems can end in a removal or a closed account. If we need more to act, we may write to the address you confirmed. If you want to add detail or check on a report, [send us a note](/contact); every note gets a reply, usually within a day.

<Callout type="info" title="A child in sexual or abusive content">

Report the photo itself and pick <UiLabel>A child in sexual or abusive content</UiLabel>. With a confirmed email, the photo can be hidden from everyone the moment you send the report, while we look, and we're told at once. Without one, your report still goes straight to the front of our queue. Nothing else comes down the instant a report arrives, so a report can't be used to wreck an album out of spite.

</Callout>
