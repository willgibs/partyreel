Most problems on an album are one photo that shouldn't be there, and the fastest fix is the person who owns the album. Reporting to Partyreel is the door for everything the host can't or won't handle.

## Ask the host first

The host has the final say over their album and can remove anything in one tap; it disappears for everyone immediately. (If you're the host, your controls are in [reporting content and keeping your event safe](/help/reporting-and-safety).) If it's your own photo you'd like gone and you have an account, you can also delete it yourself from your dashboard; see [save an event, and find your uploads later](/help/save-an-event-and-find-your-uploads).

## Report to Partyreel

At the very bottom of the event page, once you're past any password, is a small <UiLabel>Report</UiLabel> link.

<Steps>
<Step title="Tap Report">
A short form opens: <UiLabel>Report this event</UiLabel>.
</Step>
<Step title="Say what's wrong">
The <UiLabel>Reason (optional)</UiLabel> box is where you tell us which photo and why. Be specific; "the third photo from the top, it's of my kid and I'd like it removed" is exactly the right kind of note.
</Step>
<Step title="Tap Submit report">
You'll see <UiLabel>Thanks. Your report has been sent for review.</UiLabel>
</Step>
</Steps>

Reports are anonymous. You don't need an account, and the host isn't told who reported.

## What happens next

Every report gets a careful look before anything comes down. Nothing is removed the instant a report arrives, so a report can't be used to wreck an album out of spite. Serious problems can end in removals or suspended accounts. If you want to add detail or check on a report, [send us a note](/contact); every note gets a reply, usually within a day.

<Callout type="info" title="The report covers the event">

The form reports the event as a whole rather than one photo, which is why the reason box matters. Name the photo in it and we'll know exactly what to look at.

</Callout>
