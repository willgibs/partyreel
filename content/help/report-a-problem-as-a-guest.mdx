Most problems on an album are one photo that shouldn't be there, and the fastest fix is the person who owns the album. Reporting to Partyreel is the door for harm the host can't or won't handle.

## Ask the host first

The host has the final say over their album and can remove anything in one tap; it disappears for everyone immediately. (If you're the host, your controls are in [reporting content and keeping your event safe](/help/reporting-and-safety).) If it's your own photo you'd like gone, you can also delete it yourself, right on the album, and it's gone for good, for the host too; see [find your uploads, and the events you added to](/help/find-your-uploads-and-events).

## Report to Partyreel

At the very bottom of the event page, once you're past any password, is a small <UiLabel>Report</UiLabel> link.

<Steps>
<Step title="Tap Report" screen="report-open">
A short form opens: <UiLabel>Report this event</UiLabel>.
</Step>
<Step title="Say what's wrong" screen="report-reason">
The <UiLabel>Reason (optional)</UiLabel> box is where you tell us which photo and what harm it does. Be specific; "the third photo from the top is of my child, and nobody asked us before posting it" is exactly the right kind of note.
</Step>
<Step title="Tap Submit report" screen="report-sent">
You'll see <UiLabel>Thanks. Your report has been sent for review.</UiLabel>
</Step>
</Steps>

Reports are anonymous. You don't need an account, and the host isn't told who reported.

## What happens next

Every report gets a careful look before anything comes down, and we act where the harm is clear. A report that doesn't show it, or one about a photo that's simply unflattering, is closed without removing anything, so say what's wrong and why. Nothing is removed the instant a report arrives, so a report can't be used to wreck an album out of spite. Serious problems can end in a removal or a closed account. If you want to add detail or check on a report, [send us a note](/contact); every note gets a reply, usually within a day.

<Callout type="info" title="The report covers the event">

The form reports the event as a whole rather than one photo, which is why the reason box matters. Name the photo in it and we'll know exactly what to look at.

</Callout>
