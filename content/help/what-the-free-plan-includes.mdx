The free plan is a real plan, not a trial: it runs a real event, for real guests, and never expires on its own. It's sized for one gathering.

## What's included

- **<MaxEvents tier="free" />** at a time. Deleting an event frees the slot.
- **<FreeStorage /> of storage**, which comfortably holds a few hundred full-quality phone photos.
- **Photos only.** Guests (and you) can't upload video on a free event; the page says so if someone tries.
- **Everything about the album**: the live gallery, review, hide and remove, downloads for you and your guests, the guest list, and requiring accounts (which is free on every plan).
- **The highlight reel**, playing from the album's second photo in every mood, with no mark and no time limit.
- **Clips from the reel** in all 14 looks, up to <ReelSeconds tier="free" /> seconds, made at full quality with a small partyreel.com mark in the corner.

## What's paid

Four things wait behind <PlanBadge tier="paid" />: video uploads (adding a clip to the album among them), password-protected albums, a custom event link, and <ReelSeconds tier="pro" />-second clips with no mark. A public profile handle is free for everyone. Storage and the event count are the other reason to move: Pro has unlimited events and <ProPlans /> of storage; the Event Pass adds one more event with <EventPassStorage /> for <EventPassTerm />. See [Pro vs. Event Pass](/help/pro-vs-event-pass).

## The one rule to know

Events have no end date, but on the free plan an event nobody touches for <InactivityMonths /> is warned by email, then moved to Deleted, where it can be restored for <RecoveryDays />. Any activity resets the clock: signing in, a new upload, an edit. Paid plans are exempt. See [how long your media is kept](/help/how-long-media-is-kept).

## Moving up, and back

Upgrading is instant from the [pricing page](/pricing); your event, link, and QR code don't change. If you later cancel, nothing is deleted: your album stays, and if you're over the free storage you get <OverCapGraceDays /> days to make room or come back. See [upgrade, downgrade, or cancel](/help/upgrade-downgrade-or-cancel).

<Callout type="tip" title="A good way to decide">

Run your first event free. The moment you want a second event, video, or a password is the moment the paid plans are for.

</Callout>
