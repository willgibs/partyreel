The free plan is a real plan, not a trial: it runs a real event, for real guests, and never expires on its own. It's nearly everything Partyreel does, sized for a small gathering.

## What's included

- **<MaxEvents tier="free" capitalized />** at a time. Deleting an event frees the slot.
- **<FreeStorage /> of storage**, about <CapacityEstimate plan="free" />: a small gathering's worth.
- **Photos only.** Guests (and you) can't upload video on a free event; the page says so if someone tries.
- **Everything about the album**: the live gallery, review, hide and remove, downloads for you and your guests, the guest list, and asking for an email first.
- **Every door and a custom link**, exactly as on the paid plans: Public, Private behind a password, your yes at the door, an invite list or only people already in, and Only me. See [who can see your event](/help/who-can-see-your-event) and [set a custom event link](/help/custom-event-link).
- **The highlight reel**, playing from the album's second photo in every mood, with no mark and no time limit.
- **Clips from the reel** in all 14 looks, up to <ReelSeconds tier="free" /> seconds, made at full quality with a small partyreel.com mark in the corner.

## What's paid

Four things wait behind <PlanBadge tier="paid" />: video uploads (adding a clip to the album among them), more storage, clips with no mark, and, on Pro, more events. Pro has <MaxEvents tier="pro" /> and <ProPlans /> of storage; an Event Pass covers one event with <EventPassStorage /> for <EventPassTerm />, and passes stack. See [Pro vs. Event Pass](/help/pro-vs-event-pass).

## The one rule to know

No date ends an event: its date, and an end date for a run of days, only say when it happens. The one clock is the free plan's: an event nobody touches for <InactivityMonths /> is warned by email, then moved to Deleted, where it can be restored for <RecoveryDays />. Any activity resets it: signing in, a new upload, an edit. Paid plans are exempt. See [how long your media is kept](/help/how-long-media-is-kept).

## Moving up, and back

Upgrading is instant, from <UiLabel>Upgrade</UiLabel> on your account page's Plan card or from the [pricing page](/pricing); your event, link, and QR code don't change. If you later cancel, nothing is deleted: your album stays, and if you're over the free storage you get <OverCapGraceDays /> days to make room or come back. See [upgrade, downgrade, or cancel](/help/upgrade-downgrade-or-cancel).

<Callout type="tip" title="A good way to decide">

Try everything on a small event first. The moment you want video, more room, or a second event is the moment the paid plans are for.
