## Pick the Event Pass if

You're hosting one thing: a wedding, a milestone birthday, a reunion. You pay once, the event gets <EventPassStorage /> (room for a 200-guest wedding, twice over) and video for <EventPassTerm />, and there's no subscription to remember to cancel. Hosting another next year? Buy another pass; they stack, each with its own year and storage. See [how long an Event Pass lasts](/help/how-long-an-event-pass-lasts).

## Pick Pro if

You host more than a couple of times a year, or one event will outgrow <EventPassStorage />: a photographer, a venue, a club, a family that documents everything. Unlimited events, a storage size you choose (<ProPlans />: a season of parties, a planner's year, a venue's year), and yearly billing at two months free. You can change size later from <UiLabel>Change plan</UiLabel>; a smaller size just has to hold what you store. See [upgrade, downgrade, or cancel](/help/upgrade-downgrade-or-cancel).

## Moving between them

An Event Pass converts to Pro, prorated: the unused part of what you paid becomes credit against your next Pro invoices, so nothing is lost. The Pro size you pick has to hold what your passes store now; if stacked passes hold more than it does, you're shown how much before you pay. Pro holders can't buy a pass (Pro already covers it). When a pass ends and you don't renew, the account settles back to the free plan with a grace period for anything over its storage.

<Callout type="info" title="The features are identical">

Nothing about the album, the guest experience, or the reel differs between Pro and the Event Pass. Choose on events and storage alone.
