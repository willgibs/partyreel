Both paid options give you more room than the free plan — they're just shaped for different kinds of hosts.

## Pro — for people who host often

Pro is a subscription starting at <ProPrice />. It unlocks **unlimited events** and a much larger storage cap, so you can run a wedding this month, a reunion next month, and keep them all. If you're a planner, a photographer, or just someone who throws a lot of gatherings, Pro is the one.

## Event Pass — for a single big event

The Event Pass is <EventPassPrice /> with <EventPassStorage /> of storage, kept for about a year. No subscription, no recurring charge — you buy it for the occasion and that's it. Perfect for a once-a-year wedding or milestone party where you want plenty of space without an ongoing plan.

<Callout type="tip" title="A quick rule of thumb">

Hosting more than once a year? **Pro** almost always wins. Just one big event on the calendar? The **Event Pass** keeps it simple.

</Callout>

## At a glance

- **Billing** — Pro recurs monthly; the Event Pass is paid once.
- **Events** — Pro is unlimited; the Event Pass covers one.
- **Best for** — Pro for frequent hosts; the Event Pass for a single standout occasion.
