## Pick the Event Pass if

You're hosting one thing: a wedding, a milestone birthday, a reunion. You pay once, the event gets <EventPassStorage /> and video for <EventPassTerm />, and there's no subscription to remember to cancel. Hosting another next year? Buy another pass; they stack, each with its own year and storage. See [how long an Event Pass lasts](/help/how-long-an-event-pass-lasts).

## Pick Pro if

You host more than a couple of times a year, or one event will outgrow <EventPassStorage />: a photographer, a venue, a club, a family that documents everything. Unlimited events, a storage size you choose (<ProPlans />), and yearly billing at two months free. Changing size later takes one click in the billing portal. See [upgrade, downgrade, or cancel](/help/upgrade-downgrade-or-cancel).

## Moving between them

An Event Pass converts to Pro, prorated: the unused part of what you paid becomes credit against your next Pro invoices, so nothing is lost. Pro holders can't buy a pass (Pro already covers it). When a pass ends and you don't renew, the account settles back to the free plan with a grace period for anything over its storage.

<Callout type="info" title="The features are identical">

Nothing about the album, the guest experience, or the reel differs between Pro and the Event Pass. Choose on events and storage alone.
