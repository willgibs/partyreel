As the host, you move fastest. Every control you'd want in a bad moment is one tap on your own event page, and reporting to Partyreel is the door for what a host can't handle alone.

## The host's playbook

- **Get it off the album now.** <UiLabel>Hide</UiLabel> on the tile or in the photo viewer takes a photo off the album for everyone in the same second; <UiLabel>Remove</UiLabel> sends it to Deleted. Both are reversible. See [hide, remove, and restore](/help/hide-remove-and-restore).
- **Stop the flow.** Turn on <UiLabel>Review uploads before they appear</UiLabel> so nothing new goes public until you've seen it. If it's really bad, turn off <UiLabel>Accepting uploads</UiLabel>.
- **Know who's uploading.** With <UiLabel>Require verified emails</UiLabel> on (the default), every upload has a confirmed name and, for you, an email behind it, which is a strong deterrent on its own.
- **Block someone from the event.** Open their name in <UiLabel>Guests</UiLabel>, or on one of their photos, and choose <UiLabel>Block from this event</UiLabel>. Their uploads move to Deleted in the same step and they leave the guest list; the album stays closed to them, and nothing tells them they were blocked. Blocking is free on every plan. It holds on their account and confirmed email; for a guest who only typed a name, it holds on the phone they used, so the block offers to turn on <UiLabel>Require verified emails</UiLabel> too. The people you've blocked are listed at the foot of <UiLabel>Guests</UiLabel>, where <UiLabel>Let back in</UiLabel> undoes it, with <UiLabel>Also restore their uploads</UiLabel> off unless you turn it on.
- **Lock the door.** If the link has spread further than you wanted, add a password or switch to <UiLabel>Private</UiLabel>; both take effect on the existing link instantly. See [who can see your event](/help/who-can-see-your-event).

A guest who wants their own photo gone can ask you, or delete it themselves: signed in, from any device; with a typed name, from the phone they uploaded from. Their own delete is final, for you too: it leaves your album at once and never reaches your Deleted.

## Reporting to Partyreel

Anyone viewing an album, host or guest, can report it: a small <UiLabel>Report</UiLabel> link at the bottom of the event page opens <UiLabel>Report this event</UiLabel> with an optional reason. Reports are anonymous and don't need an account. Because the report covers the event as a whole, the reason box is where to name the photo. The guest's walkthrough is in [report a problem, or get a photo of you taken down](/help/report-a-problem-as-a-guest).

## What happens to a report

Every report gets a careful look, and review comes before removal. A report never lands in a void, and nothing is taken down automatically the moment one arrives, so a report can't be used to wreck an album out of spite. Serious problems can end in removals or suspended accounts. If Partyreel removes something from your album, it shows to you as no longer available and can't be restored.

## Keeping what's needed

When a report involves something serious, the material is preserved rather than deleted so that it can be handled properly. Deleting an event doesn't erase material that's under review.
