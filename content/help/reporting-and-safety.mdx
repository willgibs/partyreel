Open uploads make events fun, but they also mean the occasional photo needs to come down. Partyreel gives both hosts and guests a fast way to deal with it.

## Hosts can remove anything, instantly

As the host you have the final say over your album. Take down any photo or video and it disappears immediately — no waiting and no approval needed. If you'd rather vet everything up front, turn on [review](/help/moderate-and-curate-your-album) so uploads wait for your OK.

## Anyone can report

If a guest spots something that shouldn't be there, they can flag it for review. Reports go to us so the content can be looked at and acted on, even if the host isn't watching the album in the moment.

<Callout type="info" title="Two layers of safety">

Host removal handles the album in real time; reporting is the backstop that brings our team in when something needs attention.

</Callout>

## When to reach out to us

For anything urgent or serious — content that may be illegal, or a safety concern beyond a single photo — [contact us](/contact) directly. We take these seriously and respond as quickly as we can.

To understand who can reach your album in the first place, see [who can see your event](/help/who-can-see-your-event).
