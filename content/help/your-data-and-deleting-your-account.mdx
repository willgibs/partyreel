Partyreel is built so leaving is as easy as arriving: your photos come back out at full quality, deletion is real, and your account is yours to close. Here's the whole picture.

## What Partyreel keeps

- **Your account**: the email you verified and the display name you picked.
- **Your events**: the albums you host, their settings, and their media.
- **Your uploads**: the photos and videos you added, attributed to your name (or to "Anonymous" if you uploaded without signing in).

That's the shape of it. There's no advertising profile and nothing resold; see the [privacy policy](/privacy) for the formal version.

## Take your photos with you

Everything downloads at the quality it was uploaded: single shots from the lightbox, or the whole album as one bundle. Hosts and guests with access to an album can both use **Download album**. The full walkthrough is in [downloading photos, videos, and albums](/help/download-photos-videos-and-albums).

<Callout type="tip" title="Download before you delete">

Deleting an event removes its media for everyone. Grab the album bundle first if anyone might still want it.

</Callout>

## Deleting photos and events

- **Hosts** can remove any photo or video, or delete a whole event. Deleted items sit in a recovery bin for 30 days (so an accidental swipe is never a disaster), then they're gone for good.
- **Guests** who want one of their own uploads taken down can ask the event's host, who can remove it instantly.

The timing details live in [how long media is kept](/help/how-long-media-is-kept).

## Deleting your account

For now, account deletion is handled personally: send us a note from the [contact page](/contact) using the email on your account, and we'll delete the account for you and confirm when it's done.

A few things to know before you ask:

- **Events you host** are deleted with the account, media included. Download anything you want to keep first.
- **Photos you added to someone else's event** live in that host's album; ask the host if you'd like them removed.
- Deletion is permanent. We can't restore a deleted account afterward.
