The short version: the album shows what the host has approved, attributed by name, and nothing else. Here's the full table.

## What everyone on the album sees

- **Approved photos and videos**, newest first, at full quality.
- **Who added each one**: the display name of a signed-in guest, the host's name with a <UiLabel>Host</UiLabel> badge, or <UiLabel>Anonymous</UiLabel>.
- **The guest list**, if the host turned it on: names and photos of signed-in contributors.
- **The reel**, once the host has shared it.
- **How many photos and contributors** the album has.

## What guests never see

- **Email addresses.** Not yours, not anyone's. The host can see the email behind a signed-in upload on their own dashboard; it never reaches the album.
- **Photos waiting for review**, hidden photos, or removed photos. They aren't dimmed or blurred for guests; they simply aren't sent to the page.
- **Like counts.** Guests can like photos, but only the host sees the totals.
- **Who follows whom.** Follower lists and counts are private to each account.
- **Anything about a locked event** beyond its name and how many photos are inside. Until the password is entered, no photos leave the server, not even a preview.
- **The host's controls, stats, or plan.** Even an error message ("this event accepts photos only") is worded so it never reveals what the host is paying for.

## The preview state

On an event that asks for an email, a visitor who hasn't confirmed one sees the newest <TeaserCount /> photos and a count of the rest. That preview is a real, deliberately capped set, not a blurred version of the whole album.

## What the host sees that you don't

The host's view of the same album adds the email behind each signed-in upload, like counts, the review queue, hidden items (dimmed, still in place), and the stats row. That's the trade of an account-required event: the host asked for a name and email behind every upload so they know who's contributing. What Partyreel itself keeps is in [your data and deleting your account](/help/your-data-and-deleting-your-account).
