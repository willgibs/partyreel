Until you say otherwise, the reel is yours alone: guests see no trace of it. Sharing puts it on the event page, and unsharing takes it straight back down.

<Path>Dashboard › Your event › Reel › Share with guests</Path>

## Share it

Once the reel has at least one moment in it, the Reel section on your event page shows a card reading "Only you can see it until you share". Tap <UiLabel>Share with guests</UiLabel>. The status flips to "Shared with guests" and the card confirms it's live. "Unshare" reverses it, instantly.

You don't need to download the reel first; the live player needs no file.

## Where guests see it

- **While uploads are open**, the reel sits as a card under the event's buttons, above the gallery.
- **Once you turn off uploads**, it moves to the top of the page as the album's opening frame; the link becomes the keepsake. See [share the album after the event](/help/share-the-album-after-the-event).

Tapping the card plays the reel full-screen. Guests get <UiLabel>Download</UiLabel> for their own MP4 and <UiLabel>Share</UiLabel>, which sends the album link (never a video link). Only people who can open the full album see it: someone still at the email step, or at a password screen, sees nothing. The guest side is in [browse the album](/help/browse-the-album).

## Changes reach them live

Edit the reel after sharing and the version on the event page updates the moment you save; there's nothing to re-share. A guest's downloaded copy is whatever was current when they downloaded; the next download picks up the new cut.

## Nothing is sent

Sharing a reel doesn't email anyone. If you want guests to know it's up, send the event link again with a line about it. See [send the event link](/help/send-the-event-link).

<Callout type="info" title="Guest downloads and your download">

A guest's <UiLabel>Download</UiLabel> uses the copy you last made, or makes one on their own device if it can. If neither works on their device, they're told to ask you to download it once first. See [download the reel as a video](/help/download-the-reel-as-a-video).

</Callout>
