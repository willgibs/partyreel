Some hosts give their event a camera of its own, the way a table of disposable cameras works at a wedding. On those events the event page's Add opens the album's camera instead of your camera roll, so everything you add is shot right there, in the moment.

## Your roll

Every guest gets a roll of shots, and the host decides how many. The number beside the shutter counts what's left, and the reel under the picture fills as you go: each shot freezes the frame you were on and the reel moves on to the next one, with the minute you took it. Tap the reel any time to open <UiLabel>Your shots</UiLabel>.

When the roll is used up, the camera says <UiLabel>That’s your roll</UiLabel> and when your shots come back, with <UiLabel>See your shots</UiLabel> one tap away.

## Photos and videos

Tap the shutter for a photo. On an event that takes videos, hold it to film instead: the shutter turns red, a ring fills around it, and the camera stops on its own at the limit. Let go to stop sooner. A video counts as one shot, the same as a photo.

The first time you film, your phone asks to use the microphone. Say no and you can still film, just without sound.

## When everyone sees them

The line under the event's name in the camera says which way this event works:

- **It develops later.** Your shots stay out of the album until the develop time, then everyone's appear together. Until then only you can see yours, in <UiLabel>Your shots</UiLabel>.
- **The host approves each one.** Your shots wait for them, like any upload on that event.
- **Every shot goes straight in.** Each one joins the album as soon as it has sent.

## Changed your mind?

In <UiLabel>Your shots</UiLabel>, a shot that isn't in the album yet has a remove button. Removing a shot frees its frame, so you can take another one. A roll allows only so many retakes, and the camera tells you when they're used up.

A shot that's already in the album is removed from the album itself, from the photo viewer, like anything else you've added. See [what guests can and can't see](/help/what-guests-can-and-cant-see).

## If the camera won't open

Your browser asks to use the camera the first time. If the answer was no, the camera says <UiLabel>The camera is off for this site</UiLabel>: allow the camera in your browser's settings, then tap <UiLabel>Try again</UiLabel>. Or tap <UiLabel>Use your phone’s camera</UiLabel> to take each shot with your phone's own camera app; it still counts on your roll.

The camera turns off whenever you leave the page or lock your phone, and turns back on when you come back.

<Callout type="tip" title="Shots keep sending while you shoot">

Each shot goes up in the background, one at a time, while you keep shooting. If your signal drops, the camera says how many didn't send and offers <UiLabel>Retry</UiLabel>, and anything still unsent is listed when you close the camera. For how uploads work everywhere else, see [join an event and add your photos](/help/how-guests-join-and-upload).

</Callout>
