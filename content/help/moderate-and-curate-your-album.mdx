You're the editor of your album. Guests supply the photos; you decide what the room sees. Every control is built to be safe to tap: hide is instant and undoable, remove waits <RecoveryDays /> before anything is gone, and review means nothing goes public until you've said so.

## The three tools

| Tool | What it does | Undo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Review** | New uploads wait for your approval before appearing. | Approve them any time. |
| **Hide** | Takes a photo off the album for guests; you still see it, dimmed. | <UiLabel>Show</UiLabel> puts it back. |
| **Remove** | Takes it off the album and sends it to Deleted. | <UiLabel>Restore</UiLabel> within <RecoveryDays />. |

Each has its own guide: [review uploads before they appear](/help/review-uploads-before-they-appear) and [hide, remove, and restore](/help/hide-remove-and-restore).

## Where the controls are

<Path>Dashboard › Your event › Gallery</Path>

- **On a desktop**, hover any tile in the gallery for three quiet controls: the heart, <UiLabel>Save</UiLabel>, and <UiLabel>Hide</UiLabel> (or <UiLabel>Show</UiLabel> on a hidden one).
- **On a phone**, tiles show the heart and <UiLabel>Save</UiLabel>; open the photo to hide it.
- **In the photo viewer**, the host row adds <UiLabel>Hide</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Remove</UiLabel>, and add-to-reel.
- **In Select mode**, act on many at once: <UiLabel>Select</UiLabel> in the gallery header, or press and hold a tile. See [select many photos at once](/help/bulk-select-and-batch-actions).
- **In the Review section**, when uploads are waiting: <UiLabel>Approve all</UiLabel> in one tap, or <UiLabel>Select</UiLabel> to sort.

## A sensible routine

For most events: leave review off, let the album fill live, and hide the odd blurry frame when you spot it. For an event with an audience (a screen in the room, a client, a family that will see everything), turn review on and approve in batches. Either way, do the fine curation the next morning in Select mode; nothing you hid or removed during the night is lost.

## What guests see meanwhile

Only approved, unhidden photos. There's no "removed" placeholder, no dimmed tile, no sign anything was curated. Your own view keeps hidden photos in place, dimmed to 30 percent opacity, so you can find them again. For a bad moment, the playbook is in [reporting content and keeping your event safe](/help/reporting-and-safety).

<Callout type="tip" title="Remove lives in the photo viewer">

The tile controls stop at Hide, so a fast thumb on a dense grid can't remove anything by accident. Remove is in the photo viewer and in Select mode, both behind a confirmation.

</Callout>
