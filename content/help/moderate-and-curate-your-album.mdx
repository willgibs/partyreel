Your album is yours. Partyreel gives you two ways to stay in control: review uploads before anyone sees them, or clean things up after the fact.

## Review uploads before they go public

Turn on **review** in your event settings and every guest upload waits for your approval before it appears in the public album. Reviewing is built to be fast: skim the queue in one scroll, then tap **Approve all** to clear it in one go. Want to be choosier? Tap **Select**, pick out the exceptions, and approve or hide them together instead of one by one.

<Callout type="warning" title="Remember to save">

Event settings don't save automatically. After you flip review on (or change any setting), click **Save changes** and you'll see a "Settings saved" confirmation.

</Callout>

## Hide or remove anything, anytime

Even with review off, you're never stuck with what's public:

- **Hide** takes something off the guest album with one tap, reversibly. Hidden items stay dimmed in your own view so you can bring them back anytime.
- **Remove** deletes it. Removed items sit in a 30-day recovery bin before they're permanently deleted, so an accidental remove isn't a disaster; see [how long your media is kept](/help/how-long-media-is-kept).

You can also long-press any photo to select a whole batch and hide, remove, or approve them together.

## A good default

- **Casual events** (parties, trips): leave review off so the album fills live and everyone can watch it grow.
- **Formal or public events** (weddings, conferences): turn review on so nothing unexpected shows up on the big screen.

Once your album is the way you want it, learn how to [download your photos and videos](/help/download-photos-videos-and-albums) or read about [who can see your event](/help/who-can-see-your-event).
