Nothing you download from Partyreel is a copy of a copy. Every file is the original that was uploaded, byte for byte, with the location metadata already stripped (for JPEG, PNG, WebP, MP4, and MOV; HEIC and a few others are kept as they arrived).

## One photo or video

Open it in the photo viewer and tap <UiLabel>Save</UiLabel>. On a desktop, the same <UiLabel>Save</UiLabel> appears when you hover a tile. The file is named after the event with a short id, and it's the full-size original. Videos download as the uploaded file, not a re-encode.

## The whole album

<Path>Dashboard › Your event › Gallery › Download</Path>

Tap <UiLabel>Download</UiLabel> in the gallery header (guests have <UiLabel>Download all</UiLabel> just above the album). The <UiLabel>Download album</UiLabel> menu lists three bundles, <UiLabel>Everything</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Photos</UiLabel>, and <UiLabel>Videos</UiLabel>, each row showing its own live count and size. Hosts also get <UiLabel>Include hidden items</UiLabel>, which appears only when there's something hidden or waiting, and changes what every row is worth. Tap a bundle and it starts right away: the row is the download, no separate confirm, and the zip streams straight to your device.

A note at the top of the screen keeps you posted while the zip gets ready, and the x on its right cancels it at any point. If a file can't be found (it was deleted after you opened the menu), the note counts what made it into your download and offers to try again for the rest. If none of it can be found, nothing downloads at all: you get <UiLabel>Nothing left to download.</UiLabel> rather than an empty file.

To download a chosen set instead, use Select mode: pick tiles, then <UiLabel>Download</UiLabel> in the bar (that bundle skips the menu and includes hidden items). See [select many photos at once](/help/bulk-select-and-batch-actions).

## A very big album

A bundle too big for one file still downloads, in parts, each part its own zip. The note walks you through them: it says which part is downloading and how many there are, a tap gets the next one, and after the last it tells you that's everything. Keep every part, because together they're the whole bundle.

## On a phone

A zip saves to the Files app on an iPhone or iPad, or to Downloads on Android, never straight into your photo library (tap the zip in Files to open it). To put a single photo in Photos, open it in the photo viewer and tap <UiLabel>Save</UiLabel>.

## What guests can download

Exactly what they can see. A guest with full access can save any single photo and bundle the whole album; hidden and unapproved items are never in their zip. A guest who added photos also gets <UiLabel>Yours</UiLabel> at the top of the menu: just the photos and videos they uploaded themselves, whether from their library or taken right there in the album. A visitor who only sees the preview can download only that preview. There's no host switch to disable downloads: if someone can open the album, they can keep it, which is what the album is for. The guest side is in [browse the album](/help/browse-the-album).

## HEIC files on Windows

iPhones shoot HEIC, and Partyreel keeps the file as it came, so a downloaded album can contain `.heic` photos. They open natively on a Mac, an iPhone, or an iPad. On Windows, install the HEIF Image Extensions from the Microsoft Store, or convert the files with any photo app that exports JPEG. Thumbnails show everywhere; the full-size view of a HEIC opens only in Safari, so on Windows or Android save the file and open it in a photo app.

## Printing your photos

Download the original and take it to any print service or photo app; the files are full resolution. Partyreel doesn't offer prints.

<Callout type="tip" title="Download before you delete">

Deleting an event removes its media for everyone, and nothing in Deleted can be downloaded. Grab the <UiLabel>Everything</UiLabel> bundle first.
