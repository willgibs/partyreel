Collecting the media is only half of it — you'll want to keep it. Partyreel lets you save the originals, at full quality, whenever you like.

## Save a photo or video

Open any item in the album to view it full-screen, then choose **Save**. The original file downloads to your device — the same resolution and quality it was uploaded in, with no re-compression.

<Callout type="tip" title="Originals, not thumbnails">

What you download is the real file the guest uploaded — a full-quality photo or video, not a shrunk-down preview.

</Callout>

## Save the highlight reel

Your [highlight reel](/help/the-highlight-reel) downloads the same way — one finished video you can keep and re-share wherever you want. Like everything else in Partyreel, it's yours to take with you.

## Browsing the album

The album is built for flipping through quickly: open an item, move between photos and videos, and save the ones you love as you go. Whether you're the host or a guest with the link, the view-and-save experience is the same.
