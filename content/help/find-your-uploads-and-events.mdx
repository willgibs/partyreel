An event link is easy to lose. The photos you add are not: once your email is confirmed, every event you've added to lives on your dashboard, and everything you've added or liked across every event lives on your profile.

## The events you added to

Adding a photo or video to an event is what keeps it. Every event you've added to appears on your dashboard among your events, marked <UiLabel>Guest</UiLabel>, and it stays for as long as you have an upload in it, even one still waiting for the host's review. Delete your last upload there and the card goes with it.

Tap the Partyreel logo, top left, from anywhere in the app. To see only the events you added to, open the <UiLabel>Show</UiLabel> menu and pick <UiLabel>Guest</UiLabel>. If a host later makes an event private, its card locks; a password-protected event opens at its password, as it does for everyone.

## Keep what you add

Right after your first photos land in an album, a card offers to keep them: <UiLabel>Confirm your email</UiLabel> takes an emailed code (or <UiLabel>Continue with Google</UiLabel>) and makes a free account. Your photos move into that account and the event comes with them, so it joins your dashboard. <UiLabel>Maybe later</UiLabel> dismisses the card. The same door is in the menu under your name at the top of the album, and on the small mark beside your name on your own photos.

## Your profile

<Path>Account menu › Your profile</Path>

Your own photos, likes, and the people you follow live on your profile page, private to you even though the rest of the page is public.

- <UiLabel>Your uploads</UiLabel> is every photo and video you've added to any event, newest first, up to your most recent 200. Tap one to open it, save it, or delete it.
- <UiLabel>Your likes</UiLabel> is every photo you've hearted, on any event.

## Uploads you made before signing in

If you added photos under a typed display name and later confirm your email on the same phone and browser, Partyreel attaches those uploads to your account, and the events they're in join your dashboard; when that happens somewhere other than the album you're looking at, it says <UiLabel>We added your uploads to your account.</UiLabel> They lose the small unverified mark and appear under <UiLabel>Your uploads</UiLabel> on your profile. This works from the same phone and browser only, because that's what Partyreel can recognize as you.

## Claim photos added under your email, from any device

If you typed your email at an event that didn't need a confirmed one, it's held quietly against those uploads until you prove it's yours, however long that takes. Confirm that same address later, on any device, and your dashboard shows a card, <UiLabel>Photos waiting for you</UiLabel>, one row per event you added photos to. Tap <UiLabel>Claim</UiLabel> on the ones that were really you, or <UiLabel>Not mine</UiLabel> on any that weren't, or tap <UiLabel>Claim all</UiLabel> to take every one in a single tap.

Tap <UiLabel>Finish</UiLabel> to apply your choices. Anything you claimed loses the unverified mark, moves under <UiLabel>Your uploads</UiLabel> on your profile, and brings its event to your dashboard. If you leave anything unclaimed, a confirmation asks before those photos and videos are permanently deleted, since leaving one unclaimed is you saying it wasn't yours; if someone else used your address without you, this is how you undo it.

## Delete one of your own uploads

Open the photo, either right on the album or from <UiLabel>Your uploads</UiLabel> on your profile, and tap delete. The confirmation reads <UiLabel>Delete this upload?</UiLabel> and explains it's removed from the event right away and permanently deleted after <RecoveryDays />. This works whether you're signed in or not: signed in, it's any upload of yours, in any event, from any device, ever; without signing in, it works only from the same phone and browser you uploaded from. Either way it's final, even for the host, who can't restore it once you've removed it. If neither applies (an unverified upload from a different phone), ask the host, who can still remove it.

If the host asks for an upload before the album opens, deleting your last upload closes the album to you again until you add another; the confirmation says so before you delete. A photo the host removes never closes it.
