Every reel is the same list of moments; the style, length, and layout decide how it feels. All of them re-cut the reel instantly, and switching is free to try.

## Style

The <UiLabel>Style</UiLabel> sheet holds 14 looks in two families.

**Eight moods** keep the photos front and center and change the grade and the motion:

| Style | The feel |
| --- | --- |
| Cinematic | Clean, slow, widescreen. The default. |
| Film | Warm, grainy, a disposable-camera evening. |
| Pulse | Punchy cuts with a flash on the beat. Parties, DJ sets. |
| Kinetic | Fast whip-slides. Sports, runs, anything with energy. |
| Editorial | A magazine spread: white space, considered pacing. |
| Sunset | Golden light sweeping across. Outdoor, festivals. |
| Noir | Black and white, high contrast. Black tie, documentary. |
| Float | Soft, dreamy, a gentle bloom. Romantic, intimate. |

**Six treatments** put the photos inside a designed frame: <UiLabel>Polaroid stack</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Film strip</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Scattered prints</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Framed gallery</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Card deck</UiLabel>, and <UiLabel>Layered parallax</UiLabel>.

## Length

The <UiLabel>Length</UiLabel> sheet offers <UiLabel>Auto</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>15s</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>30s</UiLabel>, and <UiLabel>60s</UiLabel>. Auto fits the cut to the moments you picked, up to your plan's ceiling: <ReelSeconds tier="free" /> on the free plan, <ReelSeconds tier="pro" /> with <PlanBadge tier="paid" />. The 60-second option shows an upgrade link on the free plan. Longer isn't always better; a reel people watch to the end is the one they send on.

## Cover

<UiLabel>Cover</UiLabel> picks the opening shot, which is also the poster on the event page. Choose the photo that says the whole event in one frame.

## Layout

<UiLabel>Layout</UiLabel> switches between portrait (9:16, for phones, Stories, and Reels) and landscape (16:9, for a TV or a website). Every style adapts to both, and your moments are reframed to fit; a landscape photo in a portrait reel is framed on its center rather than squeezed.

## No music, by design

The reel is silent. Picking a song for someone else's wedding is a guess Partyreel isn't going to make, and timing cuts to music is a rabbit hole; the export is a clean visual montage you can lay any track under in the app you post from.

<Callout type="tip" title="Try three, keep one">

Switching style costs nothing and nothing renders until you download. Flick through a few with the reel playing; the right one is usually obvious in ten seconds.
