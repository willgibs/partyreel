Every look plays the same photographs; it decides how they feel. The highlight reel and a clip share most of them, and switching is free to try in both.

## The eight moods

The moods keep the photos front and center and change the grade and the motion. They're the reel's looks, and a clip's too.

| Look | The feel |
| --- | --- |
| Cinematic | Clean, slow, widescreen. The default. |
| Film | Warm, grainy, a disposable-camera evening. |
| Pulse | Punchy cuts with a flash on the beat. Parties, DJ sets. |
| Kinetic | Fast whip-slides. Sports, runs, anything with energy. |
| Editorial | A magazine spread: white space, considered pacing. |
| Sunset | Golden light sweeping across. Outdoor, festivals. |
| Noir | Black and white, high contrast. Black tie, documentary. |
| Float | Soft, dreamy, a gentle bloom. Romantic, intimate. |

In the reel, the host's <UiLabel>Look</UiLabel> is where everyone starts, and anyone can pick another under <UiLabel>Style</UiLabel> in the reel's controls; that choice stays on their own device. On a big screen or a laptop, every mood fills the frame edge to edge.

## Six treatments, for clips

A clip can also wear a designed frame: <UiLabel>Polaroid stack</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Film strip</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Scattered prints</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Framed gallery</UiLabel>, <UiLabel>Card deck</UiLabel>, and <UiLabel>Layered parallax</UiLabel>. The clip maker's <UiLabel>Looks</UiLabel> show each one drawn on your own clip before you choose. See [make your own clip](/help/make-your-own-clip).

## Pace and length

The reel has no length: it loops as long as the album lasts. Its pace is how long each photo holds (<UiLabel>Each photo holds</UiLabel>, in the reel's controls), which the host sets for everyone (3 seconds unless they choose) and anyone can change on their own screen.

A clip has a length. In the clip maker's tray, <UiLabel>Length</UiLabel> offers <UiLabel>Auto</UiLabel>, which fits the clip to the moments you picked, or 15, 30, or 60 seconds, up to your event's ceiling: <ReelSeconds tier="free" /> seconds on a free event, <ReelSeconds tier="pro" /> seconds with <PlanBadge tier="paid" />. On a free event the longer option stays locked, marked <UiLabel>Pro events</UiLabel> (the host sees <UiLabel>With Pro</UiLabel>). Longer isn't always better; a clip people watch to the end is the one they send on.

## Layout

<UiLabel>Layout</UiLabel> makes a clip <UiLabel>Portrait</UiLabel> (9:16, for phones, Instagram Stories and Reels, and TikTok) or <UiLabel>Landscape</UiLabel> (16:9, for a TV or a website). Every look adapts to both, and your moments are reframed to fit: a landscape photo in a portrait clip is framed on its center rather than squeezed. The reel itself follows the screen it's on.

## No music

Neither has sound. Partyreel won't guess at a song for someone else's wedding, and a silent clip is easy to score: lay any track under it in the app you post from.

<Callout type="tip" title="Try three, keep one">

Switching looks costs nothing, and nothing is made until you make it. Flick through a few with the clip playing; the right one is usually obvious in ten seconds.

</Callout>
