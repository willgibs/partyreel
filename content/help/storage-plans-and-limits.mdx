Storage is counted in bytes, not photos: the size of the original files in your albums, added up across everything you host. Guests are never counted; events are a per-plan slot count, in the table below.

## What counts

- **Every original file** in every one of your events: guest uploads and your own, photos and videos, at their uploaded size.
- **Hidden photos** and photos waiting in Review, since they're stored.
- **Anything in Deleted**, until it leaves. A removed photo or a deleted event waits <RecoveryDays /> for a possible restore and still counts while it waits; it stops counting the moment it leaves, when you empty Deleted, delete it for good, or its time runs out.
- **A clip added to the album**, which is an ordinary video once it's there. See [add a clip to the event](/help/add-a-clip-to-the-event).

## What doesn't

- **The small preview** Partyreel makes for each tile.
- **The phone-size copy** of each photo, the one guests save to their phones.
- **The highlight reel.** It plays straight from the album and is never stored as a file, and neither is a clip someone saves to their own phone.

## Uploads

Each plan also allows a set amount of uploading: everything you and your guests add, deletions included, so deleting something never gives its upload back. Free and Pro count it each calendar month; an Event Pass counts its own year, so its event can use the whole allowance in one night. It's sized so a real event doesn't meet it, which is why it sits in the pricing table beside storage. If an album ever did, its guests would see <UiLabel>This album has hit its upload limit for now.</UiLabel> until the month turns, another pass adds its own, or you move up a size.

## Per-file limits

Separately from the total, any single file can be up to <UploadSize /> on every plan, and a host can set a lower per-file cap for guests. See [turn off uploads, or cap the size of guest uploads](/help/turn-off-uploads-or-cap-file-size).

## Watching it

<Path>Dashboard › storage meter</Path>

The ring beside New event on your dashboard turns amber past 85 percent. Tap it to see your albums and Deleted drawn apart against your plan, with <UiLabel>Make room from Deleted</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>Empty Deleted</UiLabel> beside them. What happens when it fills is its own guide: [what happens when storage fills up](/help/what-happens-when-storage-fills-up).

<Callout type="info" title="Is there a guest limit?">

No. A hundred guests or a thousand, it's the same album; only what they upload counts, and a wedding's worth of phone photos fits in an Event Pass with plenty to spare.
