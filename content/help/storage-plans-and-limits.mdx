Partyreel pricing is built around one simple idea: your plan gives you a **total amount of storage**, and you fill it however you like.

## What each plan includes

- **Free** — one event and <FreeStorage /> of storage. Enough to try Partyreel on a real gathering.
- **Pro** — unlimited events and much more storage, starting at <ProPrice />. Best if you host often.
- **Event Pass** — <EventPassPrice /> for a single event with <EventPassStorage /> of space, kept for about a year. No subscription.

See the full breakdown on the [pricing page](/pricing), or compare the two paid options in [Pro vs. Event Pass](/help/pro-vs-event-pass).

## How the limit works

Your limit is a **total stored size**, not a number of photos or a number of videos. A thousand small photos and a handful of long videos draw from the same pool of gigabytes — so you're never counting items, just watching how much space you've used.

<Callout type="info" title="Per-file limits">

Whatever your plan, each upload (photo or video) can be up to <UploadSize />. That ceiling is the same on every plan, though a host can set a lower per-upload limit on an individual event.

</Callout>

## Freeing up space

Because events never expire, the way to free a slot or reclaim storage is to delete media or an entire event. Deleting is permanent and removes the underlying files — see [how long your media is kept](/help/how-long-media-is-kept) for the details.
