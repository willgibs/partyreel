Everything about how an event behaves lives on one settings page. Here are the cards, top to bottom.

<Path>Dashboard › Your event › Settings</Path>

## Details

The name, the optional description guests see when they join, and the optional date. The date is for your reference only.

## Visibility & access

<UiLabel>Who can see this album?</UiLabel> has three answers: <UiLabel>Public</UiLabel> (anyone with the link), <UiLabel>Password</UiLabel> (anyone with the link and the password), and <UiLabel>Private</UiLabel> (only you). Password protection is a <PlanBadge tier="paid" /> feature. The full model, including why "public" never means the open internet, is in [who can see your event](/help/who-can-see-your-event) and [password-protect your event](/help/password-protect-your-event).

## Guest uploads

- <UiLabel>Accepting uploads</UiLabel> turns guest uploads on or off. Off freezes the album; guests can still view it.
- <UiLabel>Max size per upload</UiLabel> caps how large any single guest upload can be. Your own uploads aren't affected.
- <UiLabel>Review uploads before they appear</UiLabel> holds new uploads for your approval.
- <UiLabel>Require accounts to upload</UiLabel> asks guests to confirm an email first. On by default, and free.
- A status line tells you whether the event accepts video, which depends on your plan.

Details: [turn off uploads, or cap the size of guest uploads](/help/turn-off-uploads-or-cap-file-size), [review uploads before they appear](/help/review-uploads-before-they-appear), and [require accounts to upload, explained](/help/require-accounts-to-upload-explained).

## Danger zone

<UiLabel>Delete event</UiLabel> removes the event and everything in it right away and frees its slot on your plan. It goes to Deleted, where you can restore it for <RecoveryDays />. There's no "end event" or "archive" option: an event is either live or deleted.

## Profile & guests

Two switches here save the moment you flip them. <UiLabel>Show on my profile</UiLabel> lists the event on your public profile page. <UiLabel>Show the guest list on the album</UiLabel> names every signed-in contributor on the album itself, to everyone who can open it, so read its description before turning it on. See [your public profile](/help/your-public-profile-following-and-blocking).

## Event link

The permanent link is always shown here. On a paid plan you can add a <UiLabel>Custom link</UiLabel>, a readable address that opens the same event. See [set a custom event link](/help/custom-event-link).

## Deleted

Appears only when you've removed something from the album: each item with its countdown, plus <UiLabel>Restore</UiLabel> and <UiLabel>Delete permanently</UiLabel>. See [hide, remove, and restore](/help/hide-remove-and-restore).

## Saving, and leaving

The Details, Visibility, and Guest uploads cards save together with <UiLabel>Save changes</UiLabel> (the button sits just above the Danger zone); a toggle you flip isn't live until you tap it. If you head back with unsaved edits, Partyreel asks <UiLabel>Discard changes?</UiLabel> before letting you go. The Profile & guests switches, the password, and the custom link each save on their own the moment you confirm them.

<Callout type="info" title="No album cover, no sort order">

There's no cover photo setting for the album page and no way to reorder the gallery; it's always newest first. The reel has its own cover and order in the Studio, the reel editor.

</Callout>
