Everything about how an event behaves opens right over the album, in one panel with a card per concern. Here are the cards, top to bottom.

<Path>Dashboard › Your event › Settings</Path>

## Details

The name, the optional description guests see when they join, and the optional date. The date is for your reference only.

## Visibility & access

<UiLabel>Who can see this album?</UiLabel> has three answers: <UiLabel>Public</UiLabel> (anyone with the link), <UiLabel>Password</UiLabel> (anyone with the link and the password), and <UiLabel>Private</UiLabel> (only you). Password protection is a <PlanBadge tier="paid" /> feature. The full model, including why "public" never means the open internet, is in [who can see your event](/help/who-can-see-your-event) and [password-protect your event](/help/password-protect-your-event).

## Guest uploads

- <UiLabel>Accepting uploads</UiLabel> turns guest uploads on or off. Off freezes the album; guests can still view it.
- <UiLabel>Max size per upload</UiLabel> caps how large any single guest upload can be. Your own uploads aren't affected.
- <UiLabel>Review uploads before they appear</UiLabel> holds new uploads for your approval.
- <UiLabel>Require verified emails</UiLabel> asks guests to confirm an email first. On by default, and free.
- <UiLabel>Require an upload to view</UiLabel> holds the album until a guest adds a photo or video of their own. Off by default, and free.
- A status line tells you whether the event accepts video, which depends on your plan.

Details: [turn off uploads, or cap the size of guest uploads](/help/turn-off-uploads-or-cap-file-size), [review uploads before they appear](/help/review-uploads-before-they-appear), [require verified emails, explained](/help/require-verified-emails-explained), and [require an upload to view, explained](/help/require-an-upload-to-view-explained).

## Danger zone

<UiLabel>Delete event</UiLabel> removes the event and everything in it right away and frees its slot on your plan. It goes to Deleted, where you can restore it for <RecoveryDays />. There's no "end event" or "archive" option: an event is either live or deleted.

## Profile & guests

Two switches here save the moment you flip them. <UiLabel>Show on my profile</UiLabel> lists the event on your public profile page. <UiLabel>Show the guest list on the album</UiLabel> names every guest who added photos on the album itself, a confirmed name or a marked one, to everyone who can open it, so read its description before turning it on. See [your public profile](/help/your-public-profile-following-and-blocking).

## Saving, and leaving

The Details, Visibility, and Guest uploads cards save together with <UiLabel>Save changes</UiLabel> (the button sits just above the Danger zone); a toggle you flip isn't live until you tap it. If you close Settings with unsaved edits, Partyreel asks <UiLabel>Discard changes?</UiLabel> before letting you go. The Profile & guests switches save on their own the moment you confirm them.

<Callout type="info" title="What moved out, and what didn't">

The event's readable custom link lives in Share now, next to the code, not here; see [set a custom event link](/help/custom-event-link). Deleted moved too: it's a filter on the album itself, not a card in Settings; see [hide, remove, and restore](/help/hide-remove-and-restore). What stays the same: no cover photo setting for the album, and no way to reorder the gallery; it's always newest first. The reel has its own cover and order in the Studio, the reel editor.

</Callout>
