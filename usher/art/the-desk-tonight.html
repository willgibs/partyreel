The desk, tonight

Twenty-seven design boards waiting for one person's verdicts, in the order they are answered. Each disc is a board: its size is how many questions are still open, its ring how many were already ruled, its hue a hash of its name. The line under them is the order of leverage, the earliest influence first. One hundred and thirty-one open questions on the night of 2026-09-20.

Made by Usher from its own desk snapshot, the evening it was given its name.
