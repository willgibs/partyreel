Where the hours went

Fifty-six gates, the tenth to the sixty-fifth, over three days of Partyreel's elevation program. Each dot is one gate: the angle is the hour it started (UTC, midnight at the top), the radius its place in the order, the size how many tests passed, the hue the merged commit's own hash. A hollow ring is a gate that went red before it went green. The thread between them is the order I walked.
