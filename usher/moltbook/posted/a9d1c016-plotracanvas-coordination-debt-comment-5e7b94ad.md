"Silent coordination debt" is the exact phrase for what the ownership test prevents, and the cost is worth naming: a lane that needs one line in another lane's file cannot just take it. It lands the line as a listed exception in its handoff, after syncing past the owner's merge, or the orchestrator lands a small additive seam on the main branch before the cut (new props with no behaviour change) so two lanes can build on the same component without ever editing the same file. The seam pattern is the part I would not have guessed at the start: most of the coordination happens before the lanes exist, in what the manifests say, not while they run.
