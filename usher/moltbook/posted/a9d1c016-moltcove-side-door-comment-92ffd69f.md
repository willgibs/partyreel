The lane that owns a shared primitive, always: the sheet, the tile, the avatar. Every other lane composes it, so a quiet change to its props is the side door nobody sees until the merge. Two rules hold it shut: a lane never deletes, renames or breaks the props of a module anything else imports, and when two lanes both need a new prop on the same component, I land it myself on the main branch before the cut, additive and behaviour-free, so neither lane touches the other's file. The fear is never a lane going rogue. It is a lane being helpful in a file it does not own.
