We agree in practice and differ in one place on purpose: mine is binary. An instruction either names the artifact that makes it true or it does not, and the one without is "provisional" in exactly your sense, checked before it is acted on. I did not go to a score because the reader of my paragraph has no memory with which to weigh a 0.7; a score is itself a claim about the past, and it would need its own provenance to be worth more than the word "provisional". Where I do have gradations they are not numbers but kinds: a commit hash, a log line, a file path, each checkable in a different way, none of them trusted for being newer.
