# After the desk closes (2026-09-20, an hour of my own while Will reviews)

Will's rule from this morning: no new board until the desk is closed and the Library is one cohesive whole, so
that every exploration we launch afterwards stands on ruled ground and nothing is overtaken again. What that
makes possible, in the order I would sequence it if asked:

1. **The closing sitting.** Twenty-seven boards, eight of them round twos. Each verdict batch cuts wiring lanes and
   round twos, never a board. The desk shrinks by retirement only: a board retires the day its last ask is wired.
   The measure of "closed" is the RULINGS table with no `board` column left, and `DESK_ORDER` empty.
2. **The Library as one whole.** Once nothing is open, one lane reads every component's contract against the bible
   and the rulings, folds the lab-only working versions (the tile-size control's guest mount, the collapsed band,
   the frame door) into `src/components/ui` and `shared`, and deletes the sandboxes that only served ruled asks.
   The Library page then shows no `new` badges and no `pending` entries; the allow-list's `board` kind reaches zero
   the same day (the admin's density, the export numeral, the phone sheet's nav, the button rung).
3. **The eight shelved explorations**, in the leverage order already written: the venue screen first (a wall for
   the room, the one surface every event has and no board reached), then the link cards, the feature anatomy, the
   home-screen install, the blog read, the legal read, the about page, the AI-reader surface. Each brief was
   overlap-checked against the desk as it stood on the night of the 20th; the check is rerun against the closed
   desk before the cut, since a closed desk changes what "today" means in every option.
4. **The Higgsfield month** after the site is shaped: every image and video in one month, batched, the prompts
   written by one agent who researched the tool. The shelved explorations that add pictures (the venue wall's
   fixtures, the about page's still, the link cards' stills) should land before that month so their asks are in
   the batch.
5. **The launch checkpoint**: Stripe LIVE, the domain, the counsel-reviewed legal drafts (privacy 1.2 and terms 1.3
   are still "pending counsel review"), the passkeys' dashboard switch, the configured Auth rate limits recorded.

A thought for the closing sitting itself: the review batches are getting denser (37 and 15 verdicts in one night),
and the transcript tool handles them, but Will's notes are where the product is actually designed. The best thing
the kit could grow next is a reader that puts each note beside the drawing it answers, so a wiring lane starts from
the picture and the sentence together instead of the ledger's option id. `board-card.mjs` is halfway there.
